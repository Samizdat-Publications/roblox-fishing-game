Judge the sound design in these clips (listen with care; quote timestamps). Score each point 0 to 10, then overall.

1. ambience: does the place sound alive and fit its time of day?
2. suspense: does the wait for a bite build tension (quiet, small sounds, a held breath)?
3. bite: does the bite land as a jolt?
4. fight: do reel, drag, line strain and splashes follow what's happening?
5. reveal: does the catch stinger feel earned for the fish's rarity?
6. mix: are levels balanced, nothing harsh, clipping, repetitive or annoying over a long session?
7. broken_or_ugly: anything missing, out of sync or wrong (10 = nothing).

Top fixes must be concrete (which sound, what to change, louder or quieter, shorter, a different character).
