Judge the interface of this fishing game (screen captures). Each screen comes at two sizes. Files ending -1280 are the desktop window: the Studio viewport as it is, at least 1280 by 720 (the real size is given with the captures). Files ending -667 are a small phone: a 667 by 375 phone screen cut out of the Device Simulator and shown at 2x (1334 by 750 pixels), so every length you measure on a -667 image is twice its size on the phone; halve it before you compare it with a pixel target. The round buttons at the top left (the Roblox logo, the menu and the chat or voice buttons) are Roblox's own top bar, not part of this game: do not score their look, but our layout must clear them, so anything of ours that sits under or touches them counts as broken. The target style is a field guide and a tackle box: paper cards (warm cream) with dark ink for anything you read, canvas and leather for the shop and the tackle box, brass for buttons, tags and the coin, a serif for titles, a clean rounded sans for body text, a handwriting face for notes; rarity colours used as an ink stamp or a ribbon. Score each point 0 to 10, then an overall score.

1. readability: can every word and number be read at a glance on the desktop size?
2. hierarchy: does the eye go first to what matters on this screen (the catch, the price, what to press), with the rest quieter?
3. consistency: do all screens share one style (shapes, colours, type, spacing), like parts of the same object?
4. handmade_feel: does it feel like a handmade field guide and tackle box, not a stock simulator kit (flat dark rounded boxes, default Gotham, bright gamer colours)?
5. contrast: is text clearly separated from its background everywhere (aim for 4.5 to 1 or better), including over the 3D scene?
6. touch_targets: are buttons and tabs big enough and far enough apart for a thumb (about 44 phone pixels or more, which is 88 pixels on the 2x -667 images)?
7. phone_readability: on the -667 phone captures only, is all text still readable (nothing under about 14 phone pixels, 28 on the 2x image), nothing cut off or overlapping, nothing under the Roblox top bar, and the scene still visible? Always score this point.
8. broken_or_ugly: anything clipped, overlapping, misaligned, off screen, under the Roblox top bar, placeholder-looking or plainly ugly (10 = nothing).

Top fixes must be concrete changes to Roblox UI (sizes in phone or desktop pixels, not image pixels, fonts, colours, panel images, layout, which element to move or remove), naming the screen and the size each fix is for.
