Judge the interface of this fishing game (screen captures). Each screen comes at two sizes: files ending -1280 are a desktop window at 1280 by 720, files ending -667 are a small phone at 667 by 375. The target style is a field guide and a tackle box: paper cards (warm cream) with dark ink for anything you read, canvas and leather for the shop and the tackle box, brass for buttons, tags and the coin, a serif for titles, a clean rounded sans for body text, a handwriting face for notes; rarity colours used as an ink stamp or a ribbon. Score each point 0 to 10, then an overall score.

1. readability: can every word and number be read at a glance on the desktop size?
2. hierarchy: does the eye go first to what matters on this screen (the catch, the price, what to press), with the rest quieter?
3. consistency: do all screens share one style (shapes, colours, type, spacing), like parts of the same object?
4. handmade_feel: does it feel like a handmade field guide and tackle box, not a stock simulator kit (flat dark rounded boxes, default Gotham, bright gamer colours)?
5. contrast: is text clearly separated from its background everywhere (aim for 4.5 to 1 or better), including over the 3D scene?
6. touch_targets: are buttons and tabs big enough and far enough apart for a thumb (about 44 pixels or more at the phone size)?
7. phone_readability: on the -667 phone captures only, is all text still readable (nothing under about 14 pixels), nothing cut off or overlapping, and the scene still visible? Always score this point.
8. broken_or_ugly: anything clipped, overlapping, misaligned, off screen, placeholder-looking or plainly ugly (10 = nothing).

Top fixes must be concrete changes to Roblox UI (sizes in pixels, fonts, colours, panel images, layout, which element to move or remove), naming the screen and the size each fix is for.
