Judge the look of this fishing place (stills and/or clips). Score each point 0 to 10, then an overall score.

1. composition: is there a clear, pleasing view toward the water?
2. light: does the light (time of day, sun, sky, shadows, haze) make it beautiful?
3. water: does the water look inviting and alive (colour, clarity, reflections, waves)?
4. mood: does it have a mood of its own, like a real lake or stream you'd remember?
5. want_to_fish: would a player want to sit and fish here?
6. phone_readability: would the scene and the interface read clearly on a small phone?
7. broken_or_ugly: anything glitched, clipping, floating, flickering or plainly ugly (10 = nothing).

Top fixes must be concrete things to change in a Roblox scene (lighting, atmosphere, water settings, props, colours, placement).
