## What the market looks like (snapshot 2026-11-25)

- Fisch and Fish It! dominate (about 64,000 to 67,000 concurrent players each, around 5 billion visits). They win on collecting: rare and mutated fish, a fish book, rod upgrades, new islands.
- Heavyweight Fishing is the best rated (about 96% liked); its draw is a real fight with big fish.
- Fishing Simulator shows that sheer content (1,000+ species) doesn't keep players by itself.

## Gaps we aim at

1. Fishing knowledge doesn't matter elsewhere. Bait and gear are tier lists; weather and time are luck boosts you can buy. Ours: real habits, right bait beats expensive bait, read the water (structure).
2. Co-op is side by side, not together. Ours (later): two-person boat, one drives or nets while the other reels.
3. Bad difficulty curves. Ours: start gentle, species-specific fights, optional assist (the Dredge approach).
4. Grind walls and reset-feeling updates. Ours: new areas build on old progress.
5. Pay-for-luck and loud shops. Ours: cosmetics only, quiet shops.
6. Trading scams. Ours: no open trading early; friends or private servers first.
7. Fish are just loot. Ours: a journal with length, weight, bait, place, time and personal bests.

## Platform rules that shape us (from Cowork's doc)

- Since the 2026 Kids and Select launch, a new game is first playable only by age-checked 16+ players and Trusted Friends. Liam can always play as a collaborator. His friends need a parent to approve the game, or the game must pass the all-ages evaluation.
- Target a Minimal or Mild rating. No homemade chat. Every control scheme from day one (PS5 Cross maps to ButtonA).
- DevEx is 13+ only, so any earnings sit on Stewart's account.
