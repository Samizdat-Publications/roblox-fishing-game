### L2. The cast (12/01 to 12/03)

Design calls are in "The new cast" below.

**In it**
- A landing ring on the water with a dotted arc from the rod tip, visible the whole time you hold. It names the spot, turns red over land and amber over a snag.
- Distance by watching the water: the ring travels out and back while you hold, at a steady pace. Let go when it sits where you want.
- One shared `CastLogic` module used by both the preview and the server, so what you see is what you get.
- Near-miss help: a ring within 3 studs of the bank slides onto the water.
- Lure weight: a spinner casts farther than live bait.
- Reasons to aim: rises, cover edges and snags.
- The feel: back swing, whip, the line arcing out, a plop and a ripple.

**A playtest must show**
- 20 casts: the server's landing point is within 1.5 studs of the ring every time (logged).
- Drop the bobber inside a 4 stud circle at 12, 24 and 36 studs at least 8 times in 10 with a mouse, and 6 in 10 on touch and gamepad.
- A rise appears. A cast within 5 studs of it inside 8 seconds gets a bite in under 4 seconds. A cast 15 studs away does not.
- The ring turns amber over a log. Landing there snags. A wiggle frees it. Live bait is sometimes lost, a lure never is.
- Tight to cover beats open water in the same spot (30 casts each, average wait logged).
- A spinner at full power outcasts a worm by the designed amount with each rod. A soft cast is still 8 studs. The riffle is still reachable with every rod. Dry-ground and Creek Pass refusals still work.

## Design calls

Made by Fable for now, per the 11/25 ruling that Claude makes the fun and look calls until Liam fills in the planner. Every number is a starting point for the playtest.

### The new cast

- **Aim** stays where the camera looks, on every device. One button, as before.
- **Distance** comes from watching the water, not a meter. While you hold, the ring moves out from 8 studs toward the rod's reach and back again at about 16 studs a second. A 4 stud target is then about half a second wide, which an 11 year old can hit.
- **What you see is what you get.** No random scatter. If wind ever bends a cast, the ring shows it first.
- **Three reasons to aim, all real fishing.**
  - *Rises.* Now and then a fish dimples the surface. Land close, quickly, and it bites fast. The server makes the rises and both players see the same ones.
  - *Cover.* Fish hold next to weeds, logs, pilings and boulders. Within 4 studs of cover bites come sooner, and cover-loving fish (bass, crappie, pike) are more likely.
  - *Snags.* Land on the log or deep in the weeds and you are stuck. Wiggle free and you may lose a worm. A lure is never lost. The ring warns in amber first, so it is a risk you chose.
- **Bait and lures have jobs.** A spinner is heavy and casts farther. A well-rigged plastic worm slides through cover without snagging. Live bait is the all-rounder.
- **From the ideas backlog:** bait you can see, lures that change the cast, a physical cast with graphics, and cast help near the bank are all in here or in L3.

### The bait bench

- **When.** Only when the hook is empty: after a fish takes live bait, or when you switch. A missed bite keeps your bait and its grade. A lure is tied once and stays tied.
- **Skipping is free.** Quick Bait, or holding to skip, gives Okay, which is exactly today's game. Doing it well only ever helps.
- **What a good job earns.** Good: bites come 10 percent sooner and the fight starts at 45 percent. Perfect: 20 percent sooner, the fight starts at 50 percent, and live bait has an even chance of surviving a catch. Nothing here changes which fish bite or how rare they are.
- **The six recipes.**

| Bait | Move | The real tip it teaches |
|---|---|---|
| Worm | Three taps as the worm slides past the point, the bend and the shank | Thread it on two or three times and leave the tail wiggling |
| Cricket | One careful tap on the collar | Hook it under the collar behind the head so it stays lively |
| Minnow | Line up on a wiggling target, then a gentle push | Up through both lips so it swims naturally |
| Stinkbait | Hold to pack, let go in the band | Pack it firm around the hook: too little flies off on the cast |
| Spinner | Tap five wraps in rhythm, then pull tight | Five wraps, through the loop, pull it snug |
| Plastic worm | In at the head, slide, bury the point | Hide the point and it will not snag |

- **Collecting.** The Tackle Box page keeps your best grade per bait and counts Perfects toward a gold hook.

### The wait

Seven things make waiting tense, and L4 builds each one.

1. You can see it coming: a shadow the size of the fish.
2. You have one small thing to do: twitch.
3. The world goes quiet just before.
4. The camera leans in and the bobber is big enough to watch.
5. False alarms: nibbles and fish that look and leave.
6. Waits are short when you chose well, and something small happens in the long ones.
7. Big fish announce themselves, and once in a long while something enormous goes by and leaves you wondering.
