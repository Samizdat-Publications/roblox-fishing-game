# Leg 4 design: the crew boat, the islands, the Survey and the cast-to-catch loop

Draft for Stewart, 11/28/2026, by the Fable advisor from Stewart's brief that night ("a large boat mechanic that can be piloted by one person with some effort but works best with a group, and conveys some kind of bonus. Then the islands with unique fish around them, and eventually a mission system to find specific fish. And most importantly the graphics and the core fishing experience."). **Not approved.** It follows leg 3 (N4 to N7) and assumes those land as planned. Milestones are P0 to P7. Nothing here is built or tested; every number is a starting value for the sim and the playtests. Gemini's leg 4 prompt (`docs/prompts/next-fable-planning-from-gemini-2026-11-27.md`) is folded in at section 4.

## Advisor's summary

- **Order:** the core loop first (draw the fight in the water), then missions (cheap, pure logic, and they teach), then a marsh island, the crew boat alone, the crew extras, and last the first slice of sea.
- **One bonus per fish, and no coin split.**
- **Rejected:** a salt ocean now (a new fish list, gear tier and economy in a leg whose priority is feel); net hauls (bulk catches flatten rarity and the per-fish reveal); a boat that needs a crew (breaks "a second player is never permission"); free walking on a moving deck (unproven on a server-owned hull).
- **How to know it worked:** a playtest clip score of 8 on the fight and the reveal; the sim shows each crew member at most 15 percent over solo and the dock kid at 31 or better; a solo Lugger run to the far island, logged; a six-dummy spread strike, logged.
- **Clear first:** old two-person code is still live: `BoatData.luau` (`crew = 2` on the Deep-V), `Teamwork.luau` (the even split), `BoatRules.luau` (drift), and the `--boats` sim model (`docs/ECONOMY.md`). These go before any crew work, with `crew = 1` everywhere.
- **Missing facts:** whether `StreamingEnabled` is on and its radii; the experience's maximum players per server; Stewart's phone frame counter.

## 1. The big boat: the Lugger

A lugger is a real type of working sailboat. The design is ours. Working shop name: Timber Lugger (Liam may rename it). Each owner picks a name for the stern board from two word lists (for example "Lucky" plus "Loon"), so there is no free text.

**The numbers**

| | The Lugger |
|---|---|
| Price | 12,000 coins: about three and a half sessions past the Deep-V for a good player (`docs/ECONOMY.md` puts the Deep-V at 70 minutes) |
| Size | 34 by 11 studs, six seats |
| Speed | 13 studs a second on the engine. The sail adds up to 9, so 22 at best. Always slower than the Deep-V's 26 |
| Handling | Turn rate 35 degrees a second, acceleration 0.8, reverse 4. Heavy on purpose, which also hides network lag |
| Water | Basin, blue water and the Reach. Not the home lake. Lives at a deep pier at the marina's east end |
| Lands at | Piers and island mooring rings only |
| Trolling lines | Two alone, one more per person aboard, six at most |

**Stations.** Every station is a seat with a rod holder beside it. You move between stations with a "Go to" prompt that takes 2 seconds, so nobody has to walk on a moving deck.

| Station | The job | Alone | With a crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helm | Wheel and throttle | "Lash the wheel" holds course and speed for 20 seconds, then eases to 5 studs a second. It always stops short of land | Someone stays on the wheel, so the boat can circle a school |
| Sheet (at the mast) | Trim the sail: stop a needle in the green, 2 to 3 seconds. Perfect, Good or Okay, as on the bait bench | Trim falls to 30 percent after any turn past 30 degrees and at each wind shift (every 90 to 150 seconds) | A deckhand taps it back every 10 to 15 seconds and trim stays above 90 percent |
| Bow lookout | A spyglass. Sees birds, schools, rises and drifting finds out to 250 studs and pings them for everyone | The helm sees signs only to 80 studs, so you leave the wheel to look | Calls marks all the time |
| Bait table | One turn at the bench rigs a tray of six baits at that grade for anyone aboard. Chum is tossed from here | Rig a tray before you leave | Someone keeps the tray full |
| Two rails | Rods, and "Net it" reaches the whole deck | | |

**The effort alone, in verbs.** Lash the wheel (one press), go to the sheet (2 seconds), trim (2 to 3), go back (2). That is about 8 seconds, each time you turn hard or the wind shifts. Skip it and you still arrive at 13 to 15 studs a second. Nothing is ever refused to a solo player.

**The group catch: the spread.** The Lugger trolls at 3 to 5 studs a second with a speed lock, the same band as leg 3's trolling. When it crosses a school, several rods bend within 3 seconds and a brass "ALL RODS!" banner shows.
- Each hooked fish holds on its rod for 20 seconds, then shakes off at no cost.
- Anyone may grab any bent rod and fight it with the normal fight.
- Alone you land one, then the second if you are quick, and the boat sails off the school.
- A crew lands one each while the helm circles back for a second strike. A school stays up for 60 seconds.

**The crew effect**

| Aboard | Passage speed | Lines | Coin bonus on fish landed aboard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 to 17 | 2 | none |
| 2 | 19 to 22 | 3 | 5 percent |
| 3 | 19 to 22 | 4 | 10 percent |
| 4 | 19 to 22 | 5 | 15 percent |
| 5 or 6 | 19 to 22 | 6 | 15 percent (the cap) |

**Money rules.**
- A crewmate counts toward the bonus if they worked a station or landed a fish in the last 3 minutes.
- The bonus is stored on the fish by the server at the catch and paid at the sale. There is no split.
- The player at the wheel during a spread strike gets the 10 percent assist on each fish landed from it.
- The game pays one assist per fish: to the netter, or else to the skipper.
- One bonus per fish, the largest. Double Hookup's bonus does not apply to spread strikes.
- The most the game ever pays on one fish is the fish plus one bonus plus one assist.

**The leg's economy budget (proposed).**
- No new water pays a solo expert more than blue water pays today (154 coins a minute by day), within 5 percent.
- Each crew member earns at most 15 percent over their solo rate in the same water.
- The dock kid earns no less than 31.
- Repeating missions pay at most 300 coins a real day.
- `tools/econ/sim.py` gains `--crew N` and runs after P4, P5 and P6.

**Kid-safe.**
- The swell is drawn on the client only.
- An empty boat goes home after 60 seconds, as now.
- If the owner leaves, the crew are set down at the Lugger pier with their bags intact.
- A player in open water for 10 seconds is put back on deck.
- The owner picks who may board: friends only (the default), anyone, or nobody.

**How it fits the code.**
- The server owns the hull as today (`LinearVelocity` and `AlignOrientation` in `BoatService`, network owner nil).
- `BoatData` gains `waterClass` (1 home, 2 basin, 3 blue, 4 Reach) in place of `blueWater`, plus `lines` and `sail`.
- `BoatLogic.step` takes a `boost` from a new pure module, `Sail.boost(wind, trim)`. `moveOk` allows engine speed plus the sail's maximum.
- Lash is server state read when the helm seat is empty.
- A new pure `Spread` module rolls strikes and hold timers. `FishingService` runs each bent rod as a normal fight.

**Art and performance.**

| Piece | Budget |
|---|---|
| Hull (a hero set) | At most 6,000 triangles, one 1024 map set |
| Sail | Three meshes (furled, full, luffing), about 300 triangles each, canvas family |
| Deck kit | At most 3,000 triangles in family materials |
| Whole boat | At most 10,000 triangles, 10 draw calls and 8 MB of textures |
| Plain twin for the phone tier | At most 4,500 triangles |
| Luggers in the world | Four slips, so at most four at once: 40,000 triangles and 40 draw calls |
| Sounds | Six, in one sheet |

## 2. The islands

**Order.** The marsh island first, then Bird Rock, then the Reach and its far island. Each uses the N6 recipe: its own ground slots, a kit of at most 12 meshes, its own light and water preset, its own fish, and a far silhouette that is always loaded. All names are working names for Liam.

**How "circling" works.** Each island has a ring of water around it and a hot arc about 70 degrees wide where its school is.
- The arc moves by one rule per island, so each island teaches one thing.
- A pure function, `IslandRing.hotAngle(island, clock, wind)`, gives the arc.
- Fish marked with that ring bite at full weight inside the arc and at 15 percent outside it.
- On screen it is 12 to 20 shadows, a dimpled surface and gulls over the arc. The lookout sees it from far off.
- The granite island's cisco gets the slowest ring: one lap a game day.

| | Reed Island (marsh) | Bird Rock | Falls Island |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | The shallows off Lily Cove, about x -380, z -420 | North-west blue water, about x -300, z -1,000 | The Reach, about x 1,500, z -600, through a 120 stud gap in the east shore (the Narrows) |
| Reached by | The canoe, or a new jon boat (800 coins, 9 studs a second, two seats, poled quietly at 4 studs a second inside the reeds). The model already exists | Deep-V or Lugger. No landing: a mooring ring | The Lugger only. About 1,620 studs from the marina: 80 seconds crewed, 105 alone |
| Landmark | A silver dead tree with an osprey nest | Two basalt stacks, a rock arch a Deep-V fits through, a cloud of gulls | A 60 stud cliff with one white waterfall facing the approach, and a cobble harbour on the lee side |
| Palette (RGB) | Reed 150,146,84. Mud 78,66,50. Water 44,58,40. Accent: lily white | Basalt 58,60,66. White 214,210,198. Water 14,44,70. Accent: lichen 196,170,60 | Cliff 96,92,88. Spruce 34,58,52. Cobble 150,146,138. Water 12,36,58. Accent: a rust-red shed 142,60,44 |
| Ring rule | Sun: shiners hold in the shade, gar bask on the sunny side | Wind: the school is pushed to the windward face | Clock: the school laps every 4 minutes, so you troll to meet it |
| It teaches | Quiet approach and sight fishing | Wind, and Light drag | The spread, and a helper at the wheel |
| New ground slots | 2 (it reuses home mud and meadow) | 3 (no plants at all) | 5 |

**The fish.** Real species with true habits. Tiers and prices come from `tools/econ/rarity_tune.py`, as in leg 2.

| Island | Common or Uncommon (the school) | Rare | Epic | Legendary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reed Island | Golden Shiner: the circling school | Longnose Gar: lies at the surface like a log at midday. It carries the bait, so you wait 2 seconds before setting the hook (a new bite style) | Bigmouth Buffalo, the hundred-year fish: bites only inside the quiet-approach minute | Alligator Gar: overcast dusk, minnows, long runs |
| Bird Rock | Alewife: the wind school | Coho Salmon: dawn, spinner, a jumper | Steelhead: overcast, at the current seam, an acrobat | King Salmon: dusk, one screaming run that needs Light drag |
| Falls Island | Striped Bass: the spread fish, with birds over it | Atlantic Salmon: the plunge pool at dawn, three leaps | Arctic Char: the deep ledge after a cold front | White Sturgeon: the cliff hole at night. With a crewmate at the wheel, line strain builds half as fast |

Existing fish (white bass, whitefish, burbot at night) fill in around each island so no water is dead. Each island keeps one empty Mythic slot for Liam's invented creature.

**Budgets.**
- **Own maps:** 12 MB per island, down from N6's 24. Three more islands at 24 would pass both the 200 MB desktop cap and the 130 MB phone cap.
- **In view at the landing shot:** 120,000 triangles on desktop, 60,000 on the phone tier, 60 draw calls.
- **Loading:** the far silhouette is at most 400 triangles and always loaded (`Persistent`, as `BoatWorld` already does). The island itself loads all at once.
- **Terrain memory:** the Reach is the largest terrain write yet. Its bed stays flat and 12 studs deep except near the island. It is measured against the 170 MB cap in P0, before it is built.

## 3. The mission system: the Survey

**Who.** A lake biologist counting fish. She has a tent and a clipboard board by the dock, and a small board at each island pier. Her lines are canned. Liam names her.

**What.** A mission is a hunt for a specific fish, with conditions and a ladder of clues.
- Each player holds at most three: one Lesson, one Request, one Crew call.
- Missions never expire.
- Daily goals stay as they are: small, daily, coins.

| Kind | How it is picked | Example | Coins |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lesson | Twelve written by hand, in order, each gated by what the player can reach | "Tag a crappie at the logs at dusk." "Land a King Salmon without a snapped line (try Light drag)." | 40 to 150, once each, about 1,000 in all |
| Request | Built from the species data: a fish the player can reach, plus one or two conditions (length, bait, time, weather, spot, boat). New each week from the week number | "A walleye at least 20 in, before a storm" | 50, 90 or 150 by tier |
| Crew call | For any boat. The target grows with the crew, so one player can finish it | "Land every fish from one spread strike" (two alone, five with four aboard) | 150 to each crew member |

**The daily cap.** One Request and one Crew call pay coins per real day. After that they pay a bait kit worth 20 to 40 coins, and the stamp. That is 300 coins a day at most and about 150 on a typical day.

**Tag and release.** Many missions ask you to release the fish for the Survey. The reward pays in place of the sale.

**How they teach.** Clues come from the same data the fish bite by, like `Daily.rumour`, so they are never wrong.
- Clue 1 is free and names the habitat.
- Clue 2 (the time) comes after 5 casts in the right water.
- Clue 3 (the bait) comes after 15.
- The Lessons cover bait, time, cover, lures, drag, hook size, weather, quiet approach, trolling, wind, the spread and the helper at the wheel.

**Journal.** A new Survey tab holds a stamped field note per Lesson and a page per island. Tagging all four of an island's fish earns its seal and a pennant for your boat, which is looks only.

**Staying fresh.**
- 44 species times the conditions, rotated weekly.
- "Beat your own best" requests.
- Island pages and crew calls.
- Below the cut line, recapture: a tagged fish can be caught again later, and the card shows how much it grew.

**Data shape.**

```lua
-- Shared/MissionData.luau (written by hand) and Shared/Missions.luau (pure rules, like Goals)
export type Need = {
	species: string?, tier: string?,
	minCm: number?, count: number?,            -- count defaults to 1
	spot: string?, island: string?, period: string?, weather: string?,
	bait: string?, boat: string?,
	release: boolean?,                          -- tag and release: the reward pays in place of the sale
	crew: { solo: number, per: number }?,       -- target = solo + per * (aboard - 1)
}
export type Mission = {
	id: string, kind: "lesson" | "request" | "crew", title: string,
	needs: { Need }, clues: { string },         -- requests build clues with Missions.clues(species)
	after: { string }?,                          -- lessons that come first
	access: { creek: boolean?, waterClass: number?, boat: string? }?,
	reward: { coins: number, bait: { [string]: number }?, stamp: string?, pennant: string? },
	teaches: string?,                            -- "bait", "time", "place", "weather", "boat", "drag", "hook", "crew"
}
-- Saved per player (save version up by one)
missions = {
	done = { [missionId] = dayNumber },
	active = { { id = "L03", progress = { 0 }, casts = 0, seed = 0 } },  -- at most three
	paidDay = 0, paidRequest = false, paidCrew = false,                  -- the daily coin cap
	tagged = { { species = "Walleye", cm = 48, day = 0, spot = "Rocks" } }, -- at most 20
}
-- Server: MissionService calls Missions.offer(data, week, access) and
-- Missions.onCatch(data, catch, { boatId, aboard, weather, island }) and pays through EconomyService.
```
