## Where it stands

**Strong.** L0 to L7 are done and proven in Studio: 355 tests, a full fishing loop with a cast ring, a bait bench, lure retrieve, line and drag, 26 fish in seven tiers, weather, frenzies, goals, a home pond and trophy wall, the Big Lake and a dinghy you can fish from. The look critic went from 3 to 7.2 out of 10 in one night.

**Five findings that shape this plan.**

1. **The game is running Roblox's "Soft" lighting style.** Roblox's own reference calls that style "A flat, retro-Roblox look with softer lights and shadows." The other style, Realistic, is the best lighting the engine has. An old note of ours says lighting can't be changed by script. That was true of the retired `Technology` property; `Lighting.LightingStyle` can be set by script (engine reference read 11/27/2026). This is the first switch to try.
2. **Nothing in the world has a material of our own.** In the saved place `MaterialService` is empty, there are 0 `SurfaceAppearance` objects, 848 of 1,163 parts are Plastic or SmoothPlastic, and the ground is stock Roblox grass and sand. The house style is "simple shapes, realistic materials", and the material half was never built: the proof corner in `art/README.md` was skipped. The 7.2 came from light, water and layout alone. This is the biggest reason the game still reads as Roblox, and the cheapest to fix.
3. **Terrain can now carry our own materials per area.** `Terrain:SetMaterialSlot` gives 62 slots, each a base material, one of our material variants and a tint (`GetMaterialSlot` answered in this Studio). That is what lets each island have its own ground, and it turns a season into a repaint.
4. **The look is set by the server for everyone at once.** `DayNight` writes Lighting four times a second, so the whole world has one water colour, a phone gets the same extras as the PC, and from a boat the blue water is the same turquoise as the shallows. Per-area water and per-device tiers need the look worked out on each player's own screen.
5. **The Big Lake has no scale.** The far shore is 265 studs from the channel mouth: 22 seconds by dinghy, and a Deep-V at 26 studs a second would cross the blue water in 6. The buoys are orange cubes and the shore is bare green domes. Boats need distance and a destination to matter.

**Smaller things found.** Wind is zero, so grass and clouds stand still. No light casts a shadow. Roblox's Open Cloud assets page (read 11/27/2026) lists audio at 100 uploads a month when ID verified and 10 when not, which is not the 2,000 we assumed: treat the audio cap as unknown until the next upload shows it. The same page lists no monthly cap for images, which is what textures are. The repo folder itself (`C:\Users\stewa\Documents\Claude\Roblox`) is still parked on the old branch `claude/overnight-duet-kpkf6f`; the build keeps working in a separate checkout of `main`, as the last leg did.

**Baseline numbers (Edit mode, 11/27/2026).** Texture memory 60 MB, terrain graphics 81 MB, 342,000 triangles and 237 draw calls in view, 16 different meshes, 7 lights.

## Visual direction: Real-ish

Real-ish in one line: **a toy-simple world shot with a real camera.** Shapes stay chunky and few. Materials, light, water and composition are true. Everything below serves that.

### The six signatures (every area follows these)

1. **Real light.** Realistic lighting on the desktop tier. The sun leads, shadows are long at dawn and golden hour, lanterns are few, warm and cast shadows. Night is moonlit navy, never black.
2. **True materials.** Nothing visible is Plastic. Every surface belongs to one of about 22 material families we made, each with a colour, a normal and a roughness map.
3. **Ground you could kneel on.** Meadow grass, forest floor under the trees, gravel on the paths, dry sand, and a dark wet band where the water meets the bank. That wet band is the cheapest "real shoreline" cue there is.
4. **Water with depth.** Pale over the shallows, dark over the deep, and different in each place: a mirror at dawn on the home lake, clear and quick in the creek, heavy navy with real swell in the blue water.
5. **A dense edge and a long view.** Trees to the waterline, then ridgelines fading into haze. Every shot has three layers: something close to frame it, the subject, and a hazed distance. No bare domes, no visible world edge.
6. **Things made by hand.** Signs are painted boards. The UI is a field guide and a tackle box. Nothing floats and nothing is a dark rounded pill.

**If you see any of these, it is wrong:** Plastic on anything visible. Neon on anything but lantern glass, running lights and the bobber tip. A floating label (button prompts excepted). A straight shore or path longer than 30 studs. Props at even spacing. Saturated primary colours outside the rarity colours and the bobber. A black night. A horizon where sky meets a bare green hill.

### The look kit (how it gets made)

**Three ways to put a material on something, cheapest first. N0 proves which hold up.**

| Layer | What | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| A. Terrain slots | Our own terrain materials through `Terrain:SetMaterialSlot`. Fallback: `MaterialService` base material overrides, one of ours per stock terrain material. | All ground and lake beds |
| B. Family materials | A `MaterialVariant` of ours on a mesh that keeps its vertex colours for hue. Models are split into at most three meshes by family (wood, cloth, metal). | Almost every prop, every tree, every fish |
| C. Hero sets | A `SurfaceAppearance` with maps baked for that one model in Blender. | The dock, both stands, the boathouse, each boat, the island beacon, the trophy case |

**Where the maps come from.** Start from CC0 photo scans (ambientCG and Poly Haven, both public domain), because a scanned plank looks more real than anything painted. Run every one through a single script, `art/scripts/texture_pipeline.py`: make it tile, size it (1024 for terrain, 512 for props, 1024 for hero sets), soften the fine noise so big grain reads and the bobber is not lost, pull the colour to the area palette, and write colour, normal (OpenGL format, as Roblox expects) and roughness. Where no scan fits (fish skin, painted wood, hypalon rubber) the modeler makes it procedurally in Blender. Every source goes in `art/textures/SOURCES.md`. Hydrogen ranks variants; the advisor picks from a material sheet (each family on a sphere, on a plank and on a patch in the game).

**The families (starting list).**
- Terrain (10): meadow grass, forest floor, shore sand, wet sand, lake mud, footpath gravel, creek pebble, wet slate, granite, deep bed.
- Props (12): weathered plank, rough timber and bark, painted wood, canvas, rope, tin roof, aluminium, hypalon rubber, gelcoat, shore stone, leaf and needle, fish skin.
- About 95 image uploads in all, each logged with its asset id in `art/textures/README.md`. Uploads are permanent, so each map is previewed before it goes up.

**The look runs on each player's screen.** A new client module, `LookDirector`, replaces the server's writing of Lighting. The server keeps the clock and the weather kind and publishes them, as now. Each client works out: the time-of-day preset, times the weather layer, times an **area preset** (home lake, creek, basin, blue water, island; blended over 40 studs as the camera moves), times a **device tier**. `LookPresets` stays the pure, tested table and gains `AREAS` and `TIERS`. The look never changes a rule: bite odds, the fog cap that keeps the bobber visible and everything else stay on the server.

**Two tiers.**

| | Desktop tier | Phone tier (touch devices, or any device that holds under 40 frames a second for 10 seconds) |
|---|---|---|
| Lighting style | Realistic | Soft |
| Shadows | Sun, plus lanterns within 60 studs | Sun only |
| Foliage | Full density, wind on | Half density past 80 studs |
| Maps | As uploaded | Hero sets fall back to family materials |
| Extras | Sun rays, bloom, soft depth of field on the bobber camera, whitecaps, 6 aurora ribbons | Bloom only, 3 ribbons |

A "Graphics: Auto, High, Low" choice goes in the journal settings.

**Life.** `workspace.GlobalWind` set by weather, so grass, clouds and particles move. Gulls over the Big Lake, a loon at dusk, pollen drifting at golden hour, leaves falling by the creek.

**Season-ready, not seasons.** Three rules now make autumn and winter cheap later: all ground goes through terrain slots (a season is a slot repaint), all foliage colours come from one palette table in `LookPresets`, and every tree's canopy is its own mesh.

### UI and signs: the field guide and the tackle box

- **Surfaces.** Paper cards (236,226,200) with ink (38,34,30) for anything you read. Canvas and leather for the shop and the tackle box. Brass (176,136,72) for buttons, tags and the coin. Rarity colours stay exactly as they are, used as an ink stamp or a ribbon.
- **Type.** A serif for titles (Merriweather first; scout confirms what Roblox's font library has), a clean rounded sans for body text, a handwriting face for journal notes and the chalkboard.
- **HUD.** A brass coin and a number. A small sun-and-moon dial for the time with the weather as a word under it. A creel for the bag. The three side buttons become tabs on the edge of a tackle box.
- **Catch card.** A field-guide plate: the fish on paper with a ruled length line, the name in the serif, the weight on a hanging tag, the rarity as a stamped seal.
- **Fight bar and cast ring.** Same shapes and sizes (they are tuned), new skins: a wood and brass frame, a water-blue track, a fish silhouette for the marker.
- **Prompts.** A brass key cap and an ink label, through the custom `ProximityPrompt` style.
- **World signs.** Painted boards on posts and eaves (a `SurfaceGui` on a wood part), lit by a lantern at night. You find the stands by their shapes and awnings, not by a label in the sky.
- **How it is built.** All colours, fonts and panel images move into one `UIStyle` module, so the restyle is one file plus images, and Liam can change it later.
- **Phone rules.** No text under 14 pixels at 667 by 375, contrast at least 4.5 to 1, touch targets at least 44 pixels.

### Who judges looks in an Opus-led run

Opus never rules on a look. ## Vehicles

### Rules for every boat

1. **Works with one, better with two.** Every job on a boat has a solo version. A second player adds speed, a second line, a net or a live instrument. A second player is never permission.
2. **Every boat is a different way to fish,** not just a faster one. Like bait: the best boat is the right boat.
3. **Every boat keeps a job** after you buy the next one, so old progress never turns useless.
4. **Real boat types, our own designs.** No brand names.
5. **The server is in charge:** it owns the hull, moves it and checks where it went. The show is drawn on each player's screen.
6. **Kid-safe.** No capsizing, sinking, fuel or damage. A boat left alone goes home. The journal gets a "Send my boat home" button.

Why a canoe: at 350 coins a dock kid owns a boat in the first session (the dinghy is about 90 minutes away for him), it is the truest "better with two" boat there is, and a cedar canoe at golden hour is the postcard for this whole style.

Why the Deep-V stays out of the home lake: at 26 studs a second it would cross the 220 stud home lake in 8 seconds. It doesn't fit under the footbridge, so it lives at a marina on the Big Lake and the home lake stays a quiet place for small boats.

### How a boat should feel

The server's hull stays flat and exact. Everything that makes it feel like a boat is drawn on the client, on the art model, so feel can never fight the rules.

- **Trim.** The bow rises up to 6 degrees as the boat passes 40 percent speed and settles to 2 or 3 on plane. It leans into turns, up to 8 degrees. The canoe rolls a little with each stroke.
- **Bob.** At rest, 0.15 studs and 1.5 degrees with the waves. Twice that in blue water.
- **Wake.** A V of two foam ribbons that widen and fade, wash at the stern, spray at the bow above 60 percent speed. The canoe leaves a hairline wake and two swirl rings per stroke.
- **Sound.** Engine pitch follows the throttle with a little lag. An idle putter. Water slapping the hull. A paddle dip and drip. A horn on H. A bell buoy you hear before you see it.
- **Camera.** Eases back and up with speed (field of view up 6 at full throttle) and returns when slow.
- **Lights.** Red and green at the bow, white at the stern, on from dusk.
- **Response.** On the 4G network preset the boat must start to turn within 200 ms of the key. If the server-owned boat can't do that at Deep-V speed, driving moves to the driver's own client with the server checking every move (`BoatLogic.moveOk`, already written).
- **Controls.** W, S, A, D. Left stick. A touch thumbstick with big Tie Up, Leave and Horn buttons.
- **Poses.** The avatar is the player's own, but sitting, paddling and holding a rod in a seat get proper poses.

### Better with two: three verbs

- **Net.** When someone within 10 studs (the same boat or the same dock) has a fish at 70 percent or more, you get "Net it". A press as the fish surfaces lands it. A miss costs nothing: try again in 2 seconds. It works everywhere, including the dock, so Dad can net Liam's first Legendary.
- **Paddle.** A second paddler takes the canoe from 7 to 11 studs a second.
- **Navigate.** In the Deep-V the second seat has the live fish finder and can drop a marker buoy both players see.

**Money on a shared catch.** The even split is gone: once a boat works with one player, a split makes two players worse off than one. The angler gets the fish at full price. The helper gets it in their journal as an assist, with a bonus of 10 percent of its price on top.

### Fishing that changes with the boat

- **Quiet approach.** Arrive under paddle, or drifting with the motor off for the last 25 studs, and wary fish (trout, muskie, pike, sturgeon and anything Rare or better that is marked wary) are 1.25 times as likely for the first minute at that spot. Arriving under power is simply normal. There is no penalty anywhere.
- **Trolling (Deep-V).** At 3 to 5 studs a second with a spinner out behind, the boat does the retrieve (the same speed band as `Retrieve`). A speed lock holds trolling speed so one person can steer and hold the rod. Lake trout and kokanee are the trolling fish. Behind `Config.TROLLING_ON`.
- **Fish finder (Deep-V).** As written in `Teamwork.finder`: marks by rarity colour and size, never the name.
- **Lily Cove.** A paddle-only cove on the Big Lake's west shore. A motor is refused with "Too shallow and weedy for a motor. Paddle in." It holds fish already in the game that like quiet weeds. No new species.

### Where boats live and where they go

- **The boathouse.** A small open shed at the dock slips with a canoe on a rack. The Boats shop moves here from Bait and Tackle, with a board for choosing which of your boats waits at your slip. The boats for sale float at a sales mooring with painted price boards, so the Deep-V is something Liam sees every session before he can afford it.
- **The marina.** On the Big Lake's south shore, east of the channel, reached on foot over the footbridge: a pier with four slips for Deep-Vs and a lantern you can steer home by.
- **The Big Lake grows north.** The basin stays as it is (x -200 to 200, z -290 to -140). Past the buoy line the lake opens out to x -450 to 450 and runs north to z -1,150 (it ended at -440). East and west shores are real banks. The north end is out of reach behind an invisible wall, with ridgeline silhouettes in haze beyond it.
- **The run.** Marina to the island (about x 100, z -950) is about 800 studs: 31 seconds at full speed, long enough to be a trip and short enough that nobody gets bored. Rock islets, the bell buoy and gulls mark the way.
- **Crossing the buoy line** is an event you feel: over 40 studs the water goes navy, the swell doubles, the wind comes up and the boat rides differently.
- **Streaming.** The island is one model that loads all at once, and its far silhouette is always loaded so it is on the horizon from the buoy line. A full-speed run must never outrun streaming (budget table).

## Rules for the unattended run

4. **Half a session.** A step that fights back for more than half a session is switched off, logged and left.

7. **Uploads are permanent.** Preview, upload once, log the id.
8. **The gate before every commit to main,** a making-of step with before and after stills after every milestone, and the morning report at the top of `docs/PROGRESS.md`.
