# Boat Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

Written overnight 2026-11-26 in the cloud, without Studio. **Everything here is unverified until the playtests below are done.** The pure data and logic for it already exist with tests (`BoatData.luau`, `BoatLogic.luau`, `BigLakeFishData.luau`, `BoatLogic.spec.luau`, `BigLakeFish.spec.luau`); they pass outside Studio with `lune run tools/test/run.luau`, and nothing in the game uses them yet.

**Goal:** A boat you buy once, board at a slip beside the dock, drive on every device, and fish from. It reaches the Big Lake, a new body of water north of the lake through a channel, with three real fish that live only there.

**Architecture:** Boat stats and rules live in pure shared modules (as rods do). Each owner's boat is a server-built model with a `VehicleSeat` for the driver and a `Seat` for a friend. A server script reads the seat's `ThrottleFloat` and `SteerFloat`, runs `BoatLogic.step`, and drives constraint movers (`LinearVelocity`, `AngularVelocity`, `AlignOrientation`, `AlignPosition`); no deprecated body movers. The server keeps physics ownership to start with (fully authoritative), and checks every boat's movement and position. Big Lake spots only count when cast from a boat, so swimming there doesn't help.

**Tech Stack:** As before (Luau, Studio MCP, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-25-fishing-game-v1.md`).

**Spec:** `docs/DESIGN.md`, later versions item 2 ("a boat"). Stewart asked Claude to make the design calls for now; the two-seat boat is groundwork for item 3 (two-person co-op).

## Global Constraints

- Server-authoritative: buying, who may drive, speed, where the boat may go, casting from it and every catch are decided on the server.
- No em dashes in player-facing text, docs or commits. Keyboard and mouse, touch and gamepad.
- Older progress stays useful: the boat adds water and fish; the lake and creek keep theirs. Data version 5 adds `boats`.
- Deprecated movers stay out: no `BodyVelocity`, `BodyGyro`, `BodyPosition`, `BodyForce`, `BodyThrust`, `BodyAngularVelocity` (Roblox lists their replacements at https://create.roblox.com/docs/physics/mover-constraints).
- Making-of step for each piece: before still first, after still when done.

## Rules (the numbers)

- **Jon Boat:** 1,500 coins, bought once, sold at Bait and Tackle under a new Boats heading (next to Rods). Top speed 24 studs a second, reverse 8, turns 70 degrees a second at speed and 35 percent of that when stopped. Two seats. All in `BoatData.luau`. The price fits after the Graphite Rod on both today's prices and the ones proposed in `docs/ECONOMY.md` (about 1 to 2 sessions for a player who knows the water, 3 to 4 at the dock).
- **Where it waits:** a boat slip on the east side of the dock walkway, around (12, 0, 62). Each owner's boat appears there when they join or buy it; four slips side by side cover a full small server, and a boat goes to the next free slip.
- **Casting from the boat:** allowed only while it moves slower than 2 studs a second (`BoatLogic.canCast`). No trolling at full speed.
- **Big Lake:** north of the lake through a 30 stud wide channel in the north shore. About 400 x 300 studs of terrain water with a small island. No path along its shores, so the boat is the way in. Three spots:
  - **Basin** (open middle): White Bass, schools at dawn and dusk, spinner or minnows; 20 to 40 cm, base price 14.
  - **Drop-off** (a steep edge along the north-east side, darker water): Lake Trout, best at dawn and by day, minnows; 40 to 90 cm, base price 40.
  - **Island point** (weedy point off the island): Muskellunge, rare, dusk and day, spinner; 60 to 130 cm, base price 90.
  - Real habits: white bass chase baitfish near the surface; lake trout hold deep and cold; muskie are rare ambushers on weedy points. All three are in `BigLakeFishData.luau` with fight profiles.
  - Note: under today's bite timer the rare muskie would bite about every 10 seconds when it's the only fish that wants a spinner at the island point. Change 1 in `docs/ECONOMY.md` fixes this (about 25 seconds); decide that first.

## Review Focus

1. Swimming or walking to the Big Lake and casting: refused ("You need a boat to fish the Big Lake."), nothing caught.
2. Buying the boat twice or without the coins: refused, coins unchanged.
3. A player who doesn't own the boat can ride in the passenger seat but can't drive it.
4. Driving onto land, through the fence, or teleporting (an exploiter moving the hull): the server puts the boat back at its last good spot.
5. Leaving the game, dying or resetting while seated: the boat goes back to the slip, empty; nobody is stuck in a seat.
6. Jump to leave the seat must not fire during a fight (the fight already turns jump keys into reeling). Leave the seat only when not fishing.
7. Old saves: no boat owned; data version 5.

## What needs checking against Roblox's docs in Studio

Read on 2026-11-26 from create.roblox.com and Roblox's creator-docs source (the cloud session couldn't open Studio):
- `VehicleSeat.ThrottleFloat` and `SteerFloat` run -1 to 1 from "forward/backward and left/right movement inputs" (https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/VehicleSeat). **Unconfirmed:** that touch thumbstick and gamepad stick feed them with no extra code. Task 3 checks this on each device.
- The VehicleSeat's own drive only works through hinge motors, which doesn't fit a hull, so the seat is read for input only and our script drives the movers.
- Network ownership: the official example gives the driver ownership while seated (https://create.roblox.com/docs/physics/network-ownership), but Roblox can't verify physics a client owns (https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/security/network-ownership). We start with the server owning the boat. If driving feels laggy in the playtest, switch to driver ownership plus the `BoatLogic.moveOk` check, and log the call in `DECISIONS.md`.
- Buoyancy: docs say water has density 1 (https://create.roblox.com/docs/physics/units) but don't describe how parts float. So the boat doesn't rely on it: an `AlignPosition` holds the hull at the water line (Y only) and an `AlignOrientation` keeps it upright. **Unconfirmed** how this feels on terrain water waves; tune in Task 3.

### Task 3: Driving on every device

**Files:** `Server/BoatService.luau` (Heartbeat drive loop), `Shared/BoatLogic.luau` (already has `step`, `moveOk`).

- Per boat, on the hull: `Attachment` at the centre; `LinearVelocity` (`VelocityConstraintMode = Plane`, `RelativeTo = World`, so the server sets the flat velocity from the heading and speed); `AngularVelocity` around Y from `yawRate`; `AlignOrientation` (`PrimaryAxisParallel`, up axis) to stay upright; `AlignPosition` limited to Y (`ForceLimitMode = PerAxis`) to sit at the water line.
- Each Heartbeat: read `ThrottleFloat`, `SteerFloat` from the VehicleSeat (0 if no driver), `BoatLogic.step`, set the movers.
- Every half second: check `BoatLogic.moveOk` and that the hull is over a water box (`FishLogic.isOnWaterAny` with the lake, channel and Big Lake boxes). If not, put it back at the last good spot and stop it.
- Leaving: jump leaves the seat when not fishing. While a bite or fight is on, the controller already eats jump keys (see `DECISIONS.md`, "Input wins the fight").

- [ ] Keyboard: W/S and A/D (and arrows) drive. Measure top speed over 3 s on the console (should be about 24 studs a second) and a full circle at speed (about 5 s for 360 degrees).
- [ ] Gamepad: left stick drives. Touch (Device Simulator, smallest phone): the thumbstick drives, the jump button leaves the seat. If either does nothing, read the stick from the default control module and write ThrottleFloat and SteerFloat from a small client script (the server still clamps them in `BoatLogic.step`).
- [ ] Drive into the dock and the shore: the boat stops, doesn't climb out. Try to drive out of the water box: put back.
- [ ] Server log shows no ownership changes (`GetNetworkOwner` is nil, the server). Note in `PROGRESS.md` how it feels; if laggy, see "What needs checking" above.
