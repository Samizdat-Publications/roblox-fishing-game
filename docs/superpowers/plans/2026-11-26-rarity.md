# Rarity Tiers, Trophies and Monsters Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every species has one of seven rarity tiers shown in its colour everywhere, and any catch can rarely be a Trophy (about 2 percent) or a Monster (about 0.2 percent) with a bigger fight, a bigger payout and a celebration.

**Architecture:** Tiers and size classes are data and pure logic in `ReplicatedStorage.Shared` (`Rarity.luau`, `FishData.tier`, `FishLogic.rollCatch`). The server rolls the size class at the hook (not after the fight) so the fight itself can be harder and the client can warn "Something huge!"; the price still comes only from the server. The client paints tier colours (catch card, journal, shadows) and plays the celebration.

**Tech Stack:** As before. **Spec:** `docs/DIRECTION.md` section 3 and order of work step 1; it wins over `DESIGN.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Rarity comes from real behaviour (spot, time, bait, later weather); nothing sold raises luck.
- Server-authoritative sizes and prices. No em dashes in player text. Every device.
- Keep the money curve: re-run `tools/econ/sim.py` with trophies and monsters and keep the 20 minute earnings within 15 percent of the pre-change run.

## Rules (the numbers)

- Tiers and colours: Common gray (175, 180, 185), Uncommon green (95, 200, 110), Rare blue (80, 150, 240), Epic purple (170, 95, 235), Legendary orange-gold (250, 175, 40), Mythic pink-red (240, 75, 125), Godlike a shifting rainbow (hue cycles every 2 s).
- Current species: Bluegill, Pumpkinseed Common; Yellow Perch, Black Crappie, Common Carp Uncommon; Largemouth Bass, Channel Catfish, Rainbow Trout, Smallmouth Bass Rare; Northern Pike, Walleye Epic. Legendary and up arrive with the species step (at least one Legendary per place).
- Size classes per catch, rolled at the hook: Monster 0.2 percent (length 1.25 to 1.5 times the species maximum), Trophy 2 percent (0.95 to 1.1 times the maximum), otherwise normal (as now). Weight follows length as today, so the payout grows naturally (a Trophy is worth about 2 to 3 times an average fish, a Monster about 5 to 8 times).
- Fights: Trophy gain x0.8 and heavy +0.15; Monster gain x0.65 and heavy +0.3 (capped at 0.9). A tracking player still lands them.
- Celebration: the catch card's border and title in the tier colour, a "TROPHY!" or "MONSTER!" banner, a camera push-in on the fish held up (1.5 s), the tier stinger (from the sound pass), a journal record mark, and for Monsters a message to everyone in the server.

## Review Focus

1. Monster and trophy rolls must use the server's random, never the client's.
2. A Monster's length can exceed `maxCm`: the journal best and pond growth must not clamp it back down (pond growth only grows toward `maxCm`, so a monster in the pond stays as is).
3. The minimum fight time check uses the adjusted gain, or a Monster win would look too fast or too slow.
4. Old saves: catches without a size class read as normal.
5. Godlike's rainbow must animate without creating a new object per frame.
