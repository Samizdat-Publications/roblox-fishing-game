**Score: 5/10**

This build already has real charm and clear fishing-game instincts: the chunky low-poly bait and fish stalls look great, the dawn and golden-hour sky reflections are genuinely pretty, and spots like the weedbed, sunken logs, and creek riffle immediately tell a player where different fish might live. Right now, though, the illusion of a real lake and stream is broken by ruler-straight rectangular shorelines and canal trenches, flat shadowless noon lighting, muddy night water, and floating sign labels clipping behind wooden roofs and arches.

Top fixes:
1. Replace the rectangular swimming-pool shorelines and straight canal trenches (07-overview-golden.jpg, 09-riffle-dawn.jpg, 10-pool-golden.jpg) with organic, curved Terrain banks: use Roblox Terrain Grow, Erode, and Smooth to carve gentle coves, shallow sloped banks, and a meandering creek bed, break up the ruler-straight beige perimeter path into curved dirt or stepping-stone trails, and cluster rocks, reeds, and overhanging trees along the water edge to hide seams.
2. Turn on grounded shadows and rebalance golden-hour and night lighting: enable Lighting.GlobalShadows with ShadowMap or Future technology so stalls, trees, docks, and fences cast soft shadows at noon (03-dockend-noon.jpg, 08-stands-noon.jpg, 11-gate-noon.jpg); dial down Atmosphere.Density and Glare at golden hour so the overview (07-overview-golden.jpg) stops washing into muddy orange fog; and at night (04-dockend-night.jpg, 12-pool-night.jpg), shorten or soften the dock lantern PointLights so they do not turn the foreground water into a flat green fog smear, add faint cool moonlight reflections, and put a small glowing neon tip or subtle PointLight on the bobber.
3. Fix all clipping BillboardGuis and refine the daytime water material: raise StudsOffset (or mount signs as SurfaceGuis directly on the wooden header boards) and set a sensible MaxDistance on Sell Fish, Bait and Tackle, and Cedar Creek so they stop clipping behind beams and overlapping from afar (01-dock-dawn.jpg, 06-logs-dawn.jpg, 08-stands-noon.jpg, 11-gate-noon.jpg); shift Terrain.WaterColor from bright pool-cyan to a deeper natural lake teal with slightly higher WaterReflectance and lower WaterTransparency; and refill/smooth the water voxel seam cutting across 10-pool-golden.jpg.

Strengths:
- Distinct, readable fishing structure that fits real fishing knowledge: foreground cattails and lily pads in the weedbed (05-weedbed-golden.jpg), boulders and fallen logs for cover (06-logs-dawn.jpg), and a rocky springhead feeding Cedar Creek (09-riffle-dawn.jpg).
- Charming, well-proportioned chunky low-poly hub props in 08-stands-noon.jpg, especially the staggered wooden dock planks, hanging fish scale, blue cooler, and fishing rod rack.
- Lovely pastel dawn and warm golden-hour sky palettes that throw appealing pink and amber ripples onto the water in 05-weedbed-golden.jpg, 06-logs-dawn.jpg, and 10-pool-golden.jpg.
- Clean, uncluttered sightline from the hub stalls down the dock to the water in 01-dock-dawn.jpg and 02-dock-golden.jpg, with cozy warm lantern pools on the dock and stands.
- High-contrast chunky silhouettes and a crisp red-and-white bobber that read easily at daytime even when scaled down to a phone screen.
