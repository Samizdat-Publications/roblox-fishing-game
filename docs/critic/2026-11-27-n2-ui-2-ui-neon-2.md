**Score: 7/10**

The field-guide and tackle-box art direction is genuinely charming and cohesive, with warm cream paper, stitched leather, brass hardware, custom illustrated icons, and clean Roblox top-bar clearance on both desktop and phone. The main things holding it back are fixable layout bugs - most noticeably the baiting bench overlapping the top HUD bar and sitting off-center, footer text colliding with stitched leather corners in the shop and bench, a clipped weight tag on the phone rare catch card, a placeholder dot-worm in the baiting minigame, and desktop-only prompts plus slightly undersized secondary text and touch targets on phone.

Top fixes:
1. Fix u06-bench (desktop 1280 & phone 667) overlap, centering, corner collision, and placeholder worm: center the bench Frame with AnchorPoint (0.5, 0.5) and Position UDim2.fromScale(0.5, 0.55) (and hide the top HUD bar while open on 667, matching Shop and Journal) so it clears the top HUD by at least 12 desktop px / 8 phone px; add UIPadding (PaddingLeft = 28 desktop px / 22 phone px, PaddingBottom = 16 desktop px / 12 phone px) so 'Tip: Thread it on...' clears the bottom-left leather corner and raise its TextSize to 14 phone px; replace the disconnected pink spheres with a single illustrated worm ImageLabel matching the HUD worm-can style.
2. Fix panel collisions, alignment, and phone clipping in u04-card-rare (667), u02-shop (1280 & 667), and u03-journal (1280 & 667): on u04-card-rare-667, increase the hanging weight tag height by 10 phone px (or use UITextSizeConstraint 12-15 phone px with two-line wrapping) so '3 lb / 5 oz' does not clip '5 oz', and shift the blue circular seal 14 phone px left (or remove it) so the RARE stamp does not cover it; on u02-shop (1280 & 667), add PaddingLeft = 36 desktop px (28 phone px) and PaddingBottom = 10 phone px to the footer label so 'You have 174 coins...' clears the bottom-left leather triangle; on u03-journal (1280 & 667), place the three bottom buttons in a centered horizontal UIListLayout with equal widths and 10 phone px bottom padding, and start the ScrollingFrame below the summary line so the scrollbar thumb aligns with the species cards.
3. Improve phone touch ergonomics, prompts, and fight readability on u01-hud (667), u05-fight (1280 & 667), and u02/u03 Close buttons (667): on 667, swap desktop prompts for touch copy ('Hold screen and let go to cast.', 'Hold anywhere to reel.', remove '(J)' and '(Q)'); increase the u01-hud left tabs and u02-shop/u03-journal Close buttons from ~37-38 phone px to at least 44 phone px tall, and bring small secondary text ('worth 22', 'Clear', shop descriptions, 'Big runs, then rests.', bench 'point/bend/shank') up to at least 14 phone px; on u05-fight (1280 & 667), shrink the oversized right-side 'REELING!' banner to a compact ~180x44 phone px toast, tighten the 'Nice!' badge to fit its text, and add a light cream (#F5ECD8) outline or lighter zone tint around the dark fish silhouette for >4.5:1 contrast.

Strengths:
- Strong handmade field-guide and tackle-box identity across almost every screen, with custom illustrated icons (fish coin, sun/moon dial, wicker creel, worm can, journal, clipboard), stitched leather headers, brass buttons, serif headings, and a tilted blue ink RARE stamp.
- Clean clearance of Roblox's top-left bar on every single desktop (-1280) and phone (-667) capture, with zero overlap or collision.
- High-contrast dark ink on warm cream paper makes primary headings, prices, counts, and catch details effortless to read over the bright sky and rippled 3D water.
- The rare catch card (u04-card-rare) has great personality and hierarchy: Largemouth Bass, the 3D specimen view with ruler, the hanging weight tag, and the RARE stamp immediately celebrate the catch while leaving half the lake visible.
- Hiding the top HUD bar inside the Shop (u02-shop-667) and Journal (u03-journal-667) on phone keeps those full-height modals uncluttered and well below the Roblox top bar.

Broken or ugly:
- u06-bench (1280 & 667): The bench panel is shifted noticeably off-center to the left and its top border overlaps the bottom of the top HUD bar (on 667, the top-right leather corner covers the 'worth 22' area).
- u06-bench (667 & 1280): 'Tip: Thread it on two or three times and leave the tail wiggling.' collides directly with the bottom-left dark brown leather corner patch on 667 and sits very close to it on 1280.
- u06-bench (1280 & 667): The worm inside the baiting minigame is drawn as disconnected pink spheres next to a plain wire hook, looking like programmer placeholder art.
- u04-card-rare (667): The hanging weight tag at top right clips out '5 oz', showing only '3 lb' instead of '3 lb 5 oz' as on desktop.
- u04-card-rare (1280 & 667): The tilted blue 'RARE' stamp overlaps and partially obscures the circular blue fish seal behind its left edge.
- u02-shop (1280 & 667): The bottom status line 'You have 174 coins. Forecast: Clear and calm.' overlaps the dark brown stitched leather corner triangle at bottom left, reducing contrast on 'You'.
- u03-journal (1280 & 667): The three bottom buttons ('Reel assist: Off', 'Quick Bait: On', 'Tackle Box') hug the bottom-left corner leaving an uneven empty gap on the right, and the scrollbar thumb sits up next to the summary line above the card list.
- u01-hud & u05-fight (667): Desktop-only keyboard and mouse instructions ('Hold click', 'Journal (J)', 'Drag: Medium (Q)', 'Hold the mouse anywhere, or Space.') appear unchanged on phone.
- u05-fight (1280 & 667): Dark fish silhouette against the muted green/dark-blue reeling bar has low contrast, 'Nice!' sits left-aligned in an overly wide paper strip, and the large 'REELING!' card covers a big chunk of the right side of the lake on phone.
- u02-shop vs u03-journal (1280 & 667): Close button styling switches inconsistently from brass with dark ink in Shop to dark stitched leather with cream text in Journal.
