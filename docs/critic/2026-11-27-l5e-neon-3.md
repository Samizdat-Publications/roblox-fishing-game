1. Reshape Cedar Creek and secondary water bodies using Roblox Terrain Smooth, Erode, and Paint tools: curve the straight rectangular banks of the riffle (09) and pool (10, 12), round out the boxy rectangular Your Pond (07), hide the straight seam line across the pool water (10, 12), and dress the bare banks with rocks, reeds, and overhanging trees.
2. Tune Lighting and Atmosphere for night and noon: at night (04, 12), darken Atmosphere.Color and Atmosphere.Decay to deep navy and lower Haze so distant hills stop glowing pale blue against the black sky, and add a small Neon tip or subtle PointLight to the bobber so players can read bites on a phone; at noon (03, 08, 11), add subtle directional shadows and light atmospheric depth so midday stops looking flat.
3. Fix signage occlusion, stall artifacts, and bare flat paths: raise or reposition the Sell Fish, Bait and Tackle, and Your Pond signs so they are not clipped behind stall roofs (01, 02) or trees (08), remove the dark horizontal z-fighting stripes on the stall sides (01, 02), and break up the sharp-edged tan path ribbons and empty lawn (01, 06, 09, 11) with Terrain sand blending, grass tufts, and shoreline props.

Strengths:
- 05-weedbed-golden and 06-logs-dawn are standout shots: the chunky cattails, pink lily pads, submerged logs, and soft pink/orange water reflections immediately suggest real fish-holding cover you want to cast into.
- The dawn and golden-hour skies (01, 02, 05, 06) give the main lake a calm, memorable mood that suits the chunky vertex-colour aesthetic.
- Big, clear silhouettes on the main dock, stalls, reeds, and distant pine hills read cleanly at desktop scale and still hold up well when shrunk to phone size.
- The overall layout in 07-overview-golden clearly separates distinct fishing micro-habitats (weedbed, dock, fallen logs) around the main lake.

- Cedar Creek's riffle (09) and pool (10, 12) have razor-sharp 90-degree rectangular banks that look like unfinished blockouts rather than a natural stream.
- A straight pale line/seam is clearly visible cutting across the left side of the pool water in both 10-pool-golden and 12-pool-night.
- Sell Fish and Bait and Tackle signs are clipped behind the stall roofs when viewed from the grass in 01-dock-dawn and 02-dock-golden (reading 'sh' and 'Bait an'), and 'Your Pond' is partially blocked by a tree in 08-stands-noon.
