**Score: 7.5/10**

The interface already nails the handmade field-guide and tackle-box identity: warm cream paper cards, stitched leather and canvas panels, wooden side tabs, brass buttons, and a charming catch card with ruler ticks and a slanted ink stamp. Every phone screen cleanly clears the Roblox top bar and keeps the water visible. What holds it back are state and layering leaks (old HUD banners peeking behind modals and colliding with the fight gauge), an off-center workbench panel with cramped bottom text and a placeholder-looking bead worm, and secondary text on the phone screens sitting just under the 14 phone-pixel legibility target.

Strengths:
- Authentic handmade style across nearly every screen: stitched canvas-and-leather Bait and Tackle shop, cream paper journal with handwritten stats, wooden-spined HUD tabs, and a standout catch card with a string-tied weight tag, ruler ticks, and stamped RARE seal.
- Clean Roblox top-bar clearance on all six phone captures (666x374) and desktop captures (1519x787), with zero UI colliding with the Roblox menu or voice buttons.
- Strong contrast over the 3D water and sky because all labels sit on opaque paper, brass, or leather backings rather than floating raw text over bright reflections.
- Generous primary phone touch targets on key actions, including the circular Fish and REEL buttons (~85 phone px), Journal bottom toggles (~46 phone px), and Hook size buttons (~45 phone px).
- Smart phone adaptation on u02-shop-667 and u03-journal-667 that hides the HUD top bar and left tabs while full-screen modals are open.
