Top fixes:
1. Break up the 90-degree water edges and paths using Roblox Terrain Sculpt and Smooth tools: curve the rectangular Cedar Creek pool (10-pool-golden.jpg, 12-pool-night.jpg), bend and widen the straight ditch-like riffle with sloped rocky/sandy banks (09-riffle-dawn.jpg), replace the rigid square sidewalk ringing the main lake (07-overview-golden.jpg) with an organic curving dirt trail, and soften the flat rectangular Your Pond outline with uneven rocks and shoreline plants.
2. Upgrade Lighting and Night Atmosphere settings: turn on crisp GlobalShadows (ShadowMap or Future) and lower OutdoorAmbient so dawn and golden hour cast real directional shadows across the grass and dock; script Atmosphere.Color and Atmosphere.Decay darker at night so distant hills stop glowing pale grey (12-pool-night.jpg); reduce Range and enable Shadows on dock and path PointLights (and switch them off during golden hour/day) to eliminate the milky green water wash in 04-dockend-night.jpg and 12-pool-night.jpg and bright ground pools in 07-overview-golden.jpg.
3. Fix clipped BillboardGui labels and refine water and night-bobber readability: raise the StudsOffset or attachment positions of the Sell Fish, Bait and Tackle, Cedar Creek, and Your Pond BillboardGuis so roofs, gate crossbars, and trees never cut them off (01-dock-dawn.jpg, 08-stands-noon.jpg, 11-gate-noon.jpg); shift Terrain.WaterColor from bright tropical cyan toward a slightly deeper, calmer lake blue-green; and add a subtle neon tip or tiny light to the bobber at night so it stays readable on desktop and small phone screens in 04-dockend-night.jpg.
