**Score: 5.5/10**

This already has the heart of a cozy, readable fishing game: the dawn and golden hour skies look genuinely lovely, the chunky low-poly stands and dock have charm, and fishing spots like the weedbed and sunken logs read immediately. What holds it back most right now is that the lake, creek riffle, and pool are built as rigid 90-degree rectangles with ruler-straight paths and a bare plank wall, which makes them feel like man-made tanks rather than memorable wild water. Fixing the boxy shorelines, smoothing out a visible water seam and harsh night lamp wash, and giving noon skies some clouds and shadows will transform the mood.

Top fixes:
1. Break up the 90-degree rectangular shorelines and straight ditches into organic banks: use Roblox Terrain Sculpt/Smooth tools (or sloped low-poly bank wedges, rocks, and bushes) to curve the square main lake (07-overview-golden.jpg), bend and vary the width of the ruler-straight riffle canal (09-riffle-dawn.jpg), and round off the rectangular pool (10-pool-golden.jpg) and dead-straight footpaths.
2. Fix the water seam, add visual depth cues, and retune night lamp lighting: remove the overlapping or misaligned water block causing the straight light-colored seam across the pool (10-pool-golden.jpg, 12-pool-night.jpg); paint darker lakebed material in deep water and lighter sand/gravel in shallows so spots read naturally; lower the Range and Brightness of the dock and path PointLights and tint them warm amber so they stop casting a milky cyan-teal wash over the dark water (04-dockend-night.jpg, 07-overview-golden.jpg, 12-pool-night.jpg).
3. Give midday skies life and replace the bare Cedar Creek wall: add a Terrain Clouds object with light wispy cover and angle the noon sun slightly off-zenith with stronger shadow contrast so midday shots (03-dockend-noon.jpg, 08-stands-noon.jpg) do not look flat; replace the long flat plank wall and mismatched posts at Cedar Creek (11-gate-noon.jpg) with a rustic wooden archway flanked by trees, boulders, and a low split-rail fence.
