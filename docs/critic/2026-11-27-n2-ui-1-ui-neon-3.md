**Score: 5.5/10**

The interface has a genuinely charming, cohesive handmade aesthetic - warm cream paper cards, stitched leather, khaki canvas, brass buttons, and tactile icons like the fish coin, sun-and-moon dial, wicker creel, and ink rarity stamp - that stands well apart from stock Roblox simulator UI. However, almost every screen is currently held back by layout, anchoring, padding, and scaling bugs: the Rare catch card and REELING banner clip off the right edge on desktop, the HUD cast prompt stays visible and collides with the fight minigame, journal entry text crosses its own card borders, bench text overlaps dark leather corners, and phone text shrinks well below 14px while modals collide with the top HUD bar.

Top fixes:
1. Fix off-screen clipping, HUD collisions, and stale prompts across u04-card-rare-1280, u05-fight-1280/667, and u01-hud-1280/667: set the Catch Card AnchorPoint to (1, 0.5) and Position to UDim2.new(1, -24, 0.5, 0) so the 3 lb tag, 18.1 in length, and RARE stamp stop clipping off-screen on 1280, and populate the blank fish portrait frame. Set the bottom HUD cast-hint Frame Visible = false during fights so it stops overlapping the reel panel (1280) and Drag button (667), anchor the REELING banner at (1, 0.5) with -24px right margin and <=300px width on 1280, and give the Drag button 52px height (44px+ on 667) with 6px top/bottom UIPadding so 'Line 8 lb' sits on the brass face instead of the dark bottom bevel. On u01-hud-1280/667, enable AutomaticSize = XY with 16px horizontal and 8px vertical UIPadding on the cast-hint box so wrapped text never spills onto water on 667, widen the three left tabs by 28px with 12px right UIPadding so 'x17', 'Journal', and '0/3' stop clipping, and raise their phone height to 44px.
2. Fix card padding, corner-patch overlaps, and modal bounds on u03-journal-1280/667, u06-bench-1280/667, and u02-shop-1280/667: on the Fish Journal, nest each species title, catch stats, and hint TextLabel inside its inset cream card Frame using a vertical UIListLayout (4px padding), UIPadding (12px top/bottom, 16px left/right), and AutomaticSize = Y so text no longer crosses the top and bottom borders. On u06-bench and u02-shop, apply >=36px horizontal and >=24px vertical UIPadding inside the canvas panel (or place leather corner ImageLabels outside the content area) so 'Bait the hook: Worms', 'Tap as the marker...', 'Tip: Thread it...', and 'You have 174 coins...' never collide with dark brown leather corners; give 'Skip' a visible button frame (min 88x44px on 667) and darken 'or hold 1 s' to #3D3226. Center the Shop and Journal modals at AnchorPoint (0.5, 0.5) and clamp their vertical bounds to leave >=12px clear below the top HUD bar on 667 and >=12px above the screen bottom on 1280.
3. Enforce a 14px minimum text size on all -667 phone captures (u02-shop-667, u03-journal-667, u04-card-rare-667, u06-bench-667): stop uniformly shrinking body text via UIScale and add UITextSizeConstraint with MinTextSize = 14 to the Journal summary and fish tips, Shop item descriptions and coin/forecast footer, Catch Card subtext, and Bench instructions/tip. Reclaim vertical space on 667 by trimming the top HUD bar height by ~8px, using 8px inner padding in scrollable lists, and removing or collapsing the secondary Tip line at the bottom of u06-bench-667.

Strengths:
- Strong handmade field-guide and tackle-box identity: the brass fish coin, sun/moon clock dial, wicker creel, worm bucket, journal, and clipboard icons look custom-crafted and immediately fit a calm fishing game.
- Cohesive palette and typography across screens: warm cream paper with dark ink, khaki canvas with stitched leather accents, brass action buttons, serif headings, and clean body copy feel like parts of one physical kit.
- Clear visual hierarchy on primary data in the top HUD bar (174 coins, 12:00 PM / Clear, 2 fish / worth 22) and shop item rows (Worms, Crickets, brass price buttons).
- Delightful physical details on the catch card (u04-card-rare-667), including the angled blue RARE ink stamp, ruler ticks below the portrait frame, and hanging 3 lb paper tag.
- The 3D water and shoreline remain visible behind the HUD, fight UI, and catch card on both 1280x720 and 667x375, preserving the calm lakeside atmosphere.
