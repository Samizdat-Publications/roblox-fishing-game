Top fixes:
1. Reshape Cedar Creek's rectangular water and soften rigid shoreline edges: use Roblox Terrain Sculpt/Erode/Smooth brushes to curve the straight 90-degree trench in 09-riffle-dawn.jpg and the sharp-cornered pool in 10-pool-golden.jpg and 12-pool-night.jpg, remove the straight seam line across the pool water, round off the boxy 'Your Pond' rectangle (07-overview-golden.jpg), and break up the uniform tan racetrack path around the main lake with irregular dirt edges, shoreline rocks, and reeds.
2. Fix night Atmosphere inversion and add crisp directional shadows: darken Lighting.Atmosphere Color, Decay, and Haze at night so distant hills silhouette naturally instead of glowing pale blue-grey against the black sky (04-dockend-night.jpg, 12-pool-night.jpg), give the bobber a faint neon tip or tiny PointLight for night readability on phones, and lower OutdoorAmbient with GlobalShadows enabled (ShadowMap or Future) so trees, stalls, and the dock cast grounding shadows at dawn, noon, and golden hour.
3. Cluster foliage to kill the flat park-yard feel and clean up obstructed/unreadable signs: group trees, bushes, tall grass, and boulders into layered clumps along the banks and distant hills instead of single evenly spaced trees (03-dockend-noon.jpg, 07-overview-golden.jpg, 11-gate-noon.jpg), raise the StudsOffset on the stall BillboardGuis so roofs stop clipping them from behind (01-dock-dawn.jpg, 02-dock-golden.jpg), move the tree blocking 'Your Pond' (08-stands-noon.jpg), and replace the illegible chalkboard scribbles (08-stands-noon.jpg) with large high-contrast text or an interactable popup.
