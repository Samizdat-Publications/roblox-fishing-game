**Score: 5/10**

The weathered timber boathouse, docks, and stalls alongside the warm golden-hour and misty dawn lighting give this fishing camp a genuinely cozy, Real-ish foundation, but the illusion breaks whenever the eye hits the flat-shaded low-poly treeline, stock Roblox shoreline terrain, default noon sky, and SmoothPlastic props. Right now it sits halfway between a bespoke Northwoods fishing game and a standard Roblox lake world, with the midday shot exposing the stock parts most clearly.

Strengths:
- Weathered timber materials on the boathouse, dock planks, pilings, and stall frames (c01-canoe-golden.jpg and c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg) read convincingly as real damp wood and nail the Real-ish goal.
- Golden-hour warmth in c01-canoe-golden.jpg and cool lavender morning mist in c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg create a calm, believable lakeside mood that already feels distinct from default daytime Roblox lighting.
- Grounded lakeside camp storytelling - the open boathouse with a hanging wooden canoe, oars, and life ring (c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg left), the wooden fish cutout atop the market stall roof, shoreline cattails, and the floating red-and-white buoy (c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg right) - starts building a real place.
- Chunky, clean architectural silhouettes on the boathouse, stalls, and dock read clearly at a glance and will hold up well on smaller phone screens.

Broken or ugly:
- In c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg (center water, between the chalkboard stall area and the boat slips), a straight line of five bare wooden pilings extends into the lake with no planks, crossbeams, or mooring ropes, reading like an unfinished or half-deleted pier.
- Across all three shots (most visible in c01-canoe-golden.jpg along slips 1-3), bright orange flat SmoothPlastic rectangles sit on top of the weathered wooden dock planks like unstyled placeholder pads or trigger mats.
- In c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg (center), the inflatable dinghy feels noticeably underscaled when occupied, leaving almost no cockpit space around the seated player and sitting right up against the bow rim.

Reads as stock Roblox:
- Low-poly pine and deciduous trees across the entire treeline (c01-canoe-golden.jpg background, c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg background and far left behind the boathouse, c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg upper shoreline): Untextured flat-shaded SmoothPlastic cones and blocky polyhedron leaf clusters read immediately as generic Roblox low-poly simulator trees and clash with the weathered wood buildings.
- Shoreline sand and grass ground (c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg mid-ground bank between the boathouse and boat slips, and c01-canoe-golden.jpg left-to-center shore): Default Roblox Terrain Sand and Grass with the recognizable soft airbrushed terrain blend rather than real wet lakeside gravel, mud, or custom MaterialVariants.
- Noon sky and cloud layer (c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg upper third; same cloud layer visible in c01-canoe-golden.jpg upper center): Flat cyan default Roblox sky with generic puffy Roblox Clouds reads immediately as stock lighting, especially at midday.
- Bright orange rectangular pads on the dock planks (c01-canoe-golden.jpg along slips 1-3 and behind the dinghy, c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg far right dock, c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg left dock): Plain untextured SmoothPlastic orange slabs sitting on realistic wood planks read as raw Roblox parts or unstyled interaction pads.
- Inflatable dinghy hull and black outboard motor (c01-canoe-golden.jpg center foreground, c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg lower right, c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg center): Despite nice chunky shaping, the untextured orange tubes and black motor cover catch uniform glossy SmoothPlastic highlights instead of reading as matte heavy rubber and painted metal.
- Shoreline rock clusters (c01-canoe-golden.jpg center bank by the cattails, c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg center bank and right bank near the dock): Dark faceted low-poly SmoothPlastic rock meshes sitting directly on stock sand terrain lack real stone grain, wetness, or moss.
- Glowing white lantern inserts (c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg two tall lamp posts behind the dock, c01-canoe-golden.jpg right dock post and left stall): Solid unshaded Neon blocks inside simple frames read as stock Roblox lighting parts rather than glass lanterns with warm light sources.
