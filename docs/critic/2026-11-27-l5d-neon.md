**Score: 6.0/10**

The main lake already has real charm at dawn and golden hour, with pretty pastel skies, inviting fishing spots like the cattail weedbed and sunken logs, and readable chunky low-poly props. What holds the scene back right now is the ruler-straight geometry around the walkways, Cedar Creek, and Your Pond, along with flat lighting that lacks grounding shadows, muddy night lighting, and several clipped in-world signs.

Top fixes:
1. Break up all ruler-straight shorelines and paths: replace the rigid rectangular walkway framing the main lake (07-overview-golden.jpg), the sharp drainage-ditch banks of Cedar Creek (09-riffle-dawn.jpg), the 90-degree corners of the creek pool (10-pool-golden.jpg, 12-pool-night.jpg), and the rectangular grey Your Pond (07-overview-golden.jpg) with curved terrain sculpting, varied shoreline boulders, sand transitions, and clusters of reeds; also remove the straight water seam in 10-pool-golden.jpg.
2. Upgrade lighting depth and fix night atmosphere: enable GlobalShadows with ShadowMap or Future lighting technology so the dock, trees, and stalls cast grounding shadows across the grass at dawn, noon, and golden hour (01-dock-dawn.jpg, 02-dock-golden.jpg, 08-stands-noon.jpg); for night (04-dockend-night.jpg, 12-pool-night.jpg), darken the Atmosphere Color and Decay so distant hills do not glow pale grey against the sky, warm up the greenish dock and pool PointLights to soft amber, and give the night bobber a subtle glow or moonlight glint for phone readability.
3. Fix clipped BillboardGuis and stall wall z-fighting: raise the StudsOffset on the Cedar Creek archway sign (11-gate-noon.jpg), the Sell Fish and Bait and Tackle stall signs (01-dock-dawn.jpg, 02-dock-golden.jpg), and the Your Pond label (08-stands-noon.jpg) so roofs, beams, and trees never cut off the text, or convert them into chunky 3D wooden boards; offset the overlapping wall parts on both wooden stalls (01-dock-dawn.jpg, 02-dock-golden.jpg) by 0.05 studs to remove the dark horizontal z-fighting lines.

Strengths:
- Dawn and golden hour skies and horizon haze (01-dock-dawn.jpg, 02-dock-golden.jpg, 05-weedbed-golden.jpg, 06-logs-dawn.jpg) create a genuinely peaceful lakeside mood and cast lovely warm and pastel reflections onto the water.
- The main lake fishing spots visually telegraph real fishing structure: the cattail and lily-pad weedbed (05-weedbed-golden.jpg), the sunken logs and rocks (06-logs-dawn.jpg), and the central dock (07-overview-golden.jpg) make a player immediately think about where fish would hold.
- The chunky low-poly art style on the wooden dock planks, striped Bait and Tackle and Sell Fish stalls (08-stands-noon.jpg), cattails, and distant tree silhouettes reads clearly at a glance and will hold up well on both desktop and small phone screens.
