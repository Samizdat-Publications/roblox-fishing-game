**Score: 4/10**

The game already has a charming low-poly dock and bait/sell stands, pleasant dawn and golden-hour sky gradients, and smart real-fishing spot variety (weedbed, sunken logs, deep buoy, and Cedar Creek riffles and pool). Right now, though, the world reads like a square swimming pool on a floating flat slab: ruler-straight 90-degree shorelines, exposed map-edge void, sharp underwater box ledges, sparse lone trees, and oversized floating labels clipping into every structure break the calm lake illusion.

Top fixes:
1. Break up the 90-degree shorelines and hide the map-edge void: curve and roughen the lake and Cedar Creek banks with angled low-poly slopes, shoreline rocks, and varied sand/grass edges instead of straight tan paths, cap the open end of Cedar Creek in 09-riffle-dawn.jpg with boulders or a bend, and surround the outer map perimeter with layered low-poly hills and dense clusters of varied-scale pines and leafy trees so the empty sky void never shows.
2. Fix the clipping and overlapping BillboardGui labels: replace the oversized floating text boxes (Sell Fish, Bait and Tackle, Your Pond, Cedar Creek) with physical low-poly wooden signs using SurfaceGui mounted on the stall roofs and a proper gate arch, or if keeping BillboardGui, convert Size to stud units, raise StudsOffset above roofs and walls so they stop clipping into posts and awnings (08-stands-noon.jpg, 11-gate-noon.jpg), and set MaxDistance to about 45 studs so they stop stacking from across the lake (06-logs-dawn.jpg, 07-overview-golden.jpg).
3. Tune Terrain water, lakebed slopes, and Lighting/Atmosphere for a natural lake mood: shift Workspace.Terrain.WaterColor from bright cyan to a deeper natural lake teal-olive with slightly lower WaterTransparency (~0.4), slope the underwater floor to erase the sharp rectangular swimming-pool step visible in 05-weedbed-golden.jpg, 06-logs-dawn.jpg, and 10-pool-golden.jpg, add a Lighting.Atmosphere (Density ~0.3 with soft Glare/Haze), lower the Range/Brightness and warm the Color on the dock lantern PointLights to remove the harsh cyan pool glow in 04-dockend-night.jpg and 07-overview-golden.jpg, and raise night OutdoorAmbient slightly with cool moonlight so 12-pool-night.jpg stays readable.

Strengths:
- The chunky low-poly wooden dock planks and striped Bait and Tackle and Sell Fish stalls in 08-stands-noon.jpg look genuinely charming and fit the art direction well.
- Dawn and golden-hour sky gradients in 01-dock-dawn.jpg, 02-dock-golden.jpg, and 05-weedbed-golden.jpg cast appealing soft pink and peach reflections across the water.
- Distinct fishing spots - cattail weedbeds with lily pads (05-weedbed-golden.jpg), half-submerged logs (06-logs-dawn.jpg), a deep-water buoy (03-dockend-noon.jpg), and a riffle/pool creek (09-riffle-dawn.jpg, 10-pool-golden.jpg) - clearly communicate where different fish should live.
- The dock entry framed symmetrically between the two stands in 01-dock-dawn.jpg and 02-dock-golden.jpg creates a clear, welcoming path straight to the water.
