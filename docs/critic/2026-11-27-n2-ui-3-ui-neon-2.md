**Score: 7/10**

The interface has a genuinely charming field-guide and tackle-box identity that clears the Roblox top bar on every desktop and phone capture. Warm cream paper cards, stitched leather, canvas, brass buttons, custom icons, and the catch card's hanging weight tag and ink stamp feel handmade and cohesive. What holds it back are a few concrete layout bugs (footer and tip text colliding with leather corner patches on the shop and bait bench, the bait bench sitting noticeably left of center), desktop-only mouse/key prompts still appearing on phone captures, slightly undersized text and buttons on the 667x375 phone size, and a placeholder-looking dotted worm graphic on the bait bench.

Strengths:
- Every screen cleanly clears the Roblox top bar on both 1280 desktop and 667 phone captures, and modals smartly hide the top HUD strip on phone to preserve vertical space.
- Strong handmade field-guide and tackle-box art direction across u01-hud, u02-shop, u03-journal, and u04-card-rare: warm cream paper with dark ink, stitched leather headers and corner patches on canvas, brass buttons, serif titles, rounded sans body text, and handwriting accents.
- u04-card-rare is a standout piece of UI, combining a hanging string weight tag, ruler ticks, tilted RARE ink stamp, circular seal, and clear hierarchy while keeping the lake and bobber visible.
- Custom drawn icons in u01-hud (brass fish coin, day/night dial, creel basket, worm bucket, field journal, checklist) read immediately at a glance and elevate the game far above stock Roblox UI.
- Main interactive areas remain uncrowded and legible over the 3D water scene because text sits on opaque warm paper rather than floating raw over bright water highlights.

Broken or ugly:
- u02-shop-1280 and u02-shop-667: footer text 'You have 174 coins. Forecast: Clear and calm.' overlaps directly onto the dark brown bottom-left leather corner patch, making 'You' / 'You ha' nearly unreadable.
- u06-bench-1280 and u06-bench-667: the entire Bait the hook panel is positioned noticeably left of center (leaving roughly twice the empty space on the right as on the left), and 'Tip: Thread it on two or three times and leave the tail wiggling.' touches/overlaps the bottom-left leather corner patch with almost zero bottom padding.
- u01-hud-667 and u05-fight-667: desktop-only mouse and keyboard instructions ('Hold click and let go to cast', 'Journal (J)', 'Drag: Medium (Q)', 'Hold the mouse anywhere, or Space.') are shown on the phone screen.
- u04-card-rare-667: the hanging '3 lb 5 oz' weight tag overlaps the tail fin of the Largemouth Bass inside the fish frame.
- u06-bench-1280 and u06-bench-667: the worm inside the baiting viewport is rendered as disconnected pink spheres/beads that look placeholder compared to the rest of the artwork.
- u03-journal-1280: the three bottom buttons ('Reel assist: Off', 'Quick Bait: On', 'Tackle Box') are left-aligned with an awkward empty gap on the right and sit almost flush against the bottom edge of the page.
- u05-fight-1280 and u05-fight-667: 'Nice!' sits left-aligned inside a wide half-empty cream strip, and on u05-fight-667 'Big runs, then rests.' nearly touches the left and right edges of the brown meter frame.
