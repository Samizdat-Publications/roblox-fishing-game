**Score: 6/10**

There is genuine charm here: the chunky low-poly stands, striped canopies, and warm dawn and golden-hour palettes already give spots like the weedbed (05-weedbed-golden.jpg) and submerged logs (06-logs-dawn.jpg) a cosy, readable fishing vibe. What holds the place back from feeling like a memorable real lake is the flat shadowless noon light, broken nighttime atmosphere and pitch-black water, a boxy lake layout ringed by a uniform beige path (07-overview-golden.jpg), and tall grass that completely hides the Cedar Creek riffle (09-riffle-dawn.jpg).

Top fixes:
1. Overhaul night and noon Lighting and Atmosphere: enable Lighting.GlobalShadows with ShadowMap or Future lighting so stands, trees, and the dock cast grounding shadows at noon (03-dockend-noon.jpg, 08-stands-noon.jpg, 11-gate-noon.jpg); at night, script Atmosphere.Color and Decay to dark navy and lower Atmosphere.Haze so distant hills stop glowing like pale cutouts (12-pool-night.jpg), and switch dock and path PointLights to warm amber with softer falloff plus subtle moonlight reflection so the water is not a black void with a greenish blotch (04-dockend-night.jpg, 12-pool-night.jpg).
2. Break up the artificial shorelines and uncover the riffle: interrupt the continuous beige loop path ringing the boxy main lake (07-overview-golden.jpg) by pulling the path back from the water and adding irregular rocky points, overhanging trees, and shoreline brush; dress the bare, smooth clay banks of the Cedar Creek pool (10-pool-golden.jpg) with low-poly boulders and roots; and clear or paint out the Terrain grass decoration right along the creek edge so the riffle water is actually visible at eye level (09-riffle-dawn.jpg).
3. Make water depth and fishing structure readable and tidy up world signage: increase Terrain.WaterTransparency slightly and paint lighter sandy shallows versus darker rocky drop-offs on the lakebed so players can read the water like real anglers; rebuild Your Pond (07-overview-golden.jpg) as an organic sculpted water basin instead of a flat purple-grey rectangle; and raise StudsOffset while capping MaxDistance on the stand BillboardGuis so roofs stop slicing them into sh and Bait an from behind (01-dock-dawn.jpg, 02-dock-golden.jpg) and they do not float across the far shore (06-logs-dawn.jpg).

Strengths:

- Lovely dawn and golden-hour sky gradients and water glints in 01-dock-dawn.jpg, 02-dock-golden.jpg, 05-weedbed-golden.jpg, and 06-logs-dawn.jpg that nail a calm morning-on-the-water mood.
- Bold, chunky low-poly silhouettes on the Bait and Tackle and Sell Fish stands, dock planks, cattails, and red buoy (03-dockend-noon.jpg, 08-stands-noon.jpg) that read cleanly on desktop and will still hold up well on a phone screen.
