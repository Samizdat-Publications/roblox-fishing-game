This already has genuinely charming low-poly props, a lovely dawn and golden-hour sky palette, and smart fishing-spot variety (dock, cattail weedbed, sunken logs, riffle, and pool) that fits a real-knowledge fishing game. 2. Refine water colour, fix the pool seam, and improve night readability: Replace the tiled water in 10-pool-golden.jpg with a single continuous water volume/part to remove the horizontal seam; shift the noon water color in 03-dockend-noon.jpg from bright tropical cyan toward a calmer, slightly darker olive-teal freshwater tone; and for night (04-dockend-night.jpg), reduce the dock lamps' PointLight range/brightness to eliminate the flat green wash on the foreground water while adding a tiny Neon tip to the bobber so bites remain readable on phones.

Fix clipping BillboardGuis and ground the scene with shadows and shoreline clutter: Increase the vertical StudsOffset (or switch to physical wooden signboards with SurfaceGui) on the Cedar Creek archway sign (11-gate-noon.jpg) and the Bait and Tackle / Sell Fish stall signs (08-stands-noon.jpg) so text stops intersecting wood beams; ensure Lighting.GlobalShadows is enabled with CastShadow active on trees, fences, and stalls; and break up the empty flat lawn in 01-dock-dawn.jpg and bare banks in 10-pool-golden.jpg with clusters of rocks, bushes, wildflowers, and wooden benches or crates.
