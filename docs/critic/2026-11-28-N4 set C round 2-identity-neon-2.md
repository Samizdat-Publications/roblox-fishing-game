**Score: 5.5/10**

The game already has genuine lakeside charm in golden hour (c01-canoe-golden.jpg) and misty dawn (c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg), carried by rich weathered wood on the boathouse, stalls, and docks plus the high-contrast orange dinghy. However, midday (c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg) exposes a clear style split: Real-ish textured timber sits in front of a generic low-poly SmoothPlastic forest, mushy stock Roblox Terrain shoreline, and a default blue sky, pulling the overall look back toward standard Roblox.

Top fixes:
1. Replace the flat-shaded SmoothPlastic pine and broadleaf trees across the background treeline (c01-canoe-golden.jpg, c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg, c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg) with Real-ish trees using a PBR bark MaterialVariant on trunks and textured layered foliage cards or detailed needle clusters with varied heights and muted greens, matching the fidelity of the wooden boathouse.
2. Override the default Terrain Sand, Mud, and Grass textures on the shoreline (c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg center, c01-canoe-golden.jpg left bank) with gritty pebble-and-earth MaterialVariants dressed with varied textured shoreline rocks, and replace the default midday Sky/Clouds in c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg with a custom skybox, subtle Atmosphere haze, and warm ColorCorrection so noon has the same mood as dawn and golden hour.
3. Replace the remaining SmoothPlastic and Neon-block prop details: apply a matte Fabric or Canvas MaterialVariant to the striped awnings on the Bait and Tackle and Sell Fish stalls (c01-canoe-golden.jpg, c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg), replace the flat bright orange plastic pads on the dock planks (c01-canoe-golden.jpg, c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg) with weathered metal cleats or painted wood deck markings, and swap the solid Neon lantern cubes (c01-canoe-golden.jpg, c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg) for semi-transparent glass panes around a warm inner bulb.

Strengths:
- Weathered wood plank and log-piling materials on the docks (c01-canoe-golden.jpg, c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg) and the dark timber boathouse with hanging canoe and oars (c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg) genuinely read as real wood.
- Golden-hour lighting in c01-canoe-golden.jpg and the cool blue-grey morning mist in c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg create a calm, believable fishing-lake mood.
- The safety-orange inflatable dinghy with rope detailing and outboard motor gives a strong focal silhouette against the dark dock and water across all three shots.
- The viewport stays completely clean of floating always-on-top name labels or default dark UI boxes, letting the lakeside hub feel grounded.

Reads as stock Roblox:
- Low-poly pine and broadleaf trees along the entire treeline (c01-canoe-golden.jpg, c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg especially far left and center background, c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg): Stacked flat-shaded cones and blobby foliage clusters in untextured SmoothPlastic/Plastic read as generic Roblox low-poly asset-pack trees and clash with the textured wood buildings.
- Shoreline ground and hills (c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg center beach between the boathouse and slips; c01-canoe-golden.jpg left bank): Soft, low-detail transitions between default Roblox Terrain Sand, Ground, and Grass immediately read as stock Terrain.
- Daytime sky and wispy clouds (c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg upper half): Bright cyan-blue default Roblox Sky with stock Clouds gives the noon shot a default Studio template look.
- Striped stall awnings on Bait and Tackle and Sell Fish (c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg center-right; c01-canoe-golden.jpg upper left): Rigid, untextured SmoothPlastic slats read as plastic blocks rather than woven canvas fabric.
- Bright orange rectangular pads on the wooden dock planks (c01-canoe-golden.jpg along the dock near slips 1 to 3; c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg far right; c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg left dock): Flat, untextured SmoothPlastic blocks sitting directly on weathered timber look like stock Roblox trigger pads.
- Glowing lantern cores on the dock posts and Sell Fish stall (c01-canoe-golden.jpg upper left and right; c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg top center): Plain solid Neon cubes inside simple frames are a classic stock Roblox lighting shortcut.
