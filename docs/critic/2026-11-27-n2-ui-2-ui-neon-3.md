**Score: 6.5/10**

The handmade field-guide and tackle-box visual identity is genuinely charming and cohesive, with warm cream paper cards, stitched leather trim, brass buttons, and illustrated icons that clearly separate the UI from stock Roblox simulators. However, several concrete layout and responsive bugs hold it back - especially on the 667 phone size - including an off-center baiting bench that overlaps the top HUD bar, a clipped fish weight tag that drops '5 oz', footer text colliding with leather corner patches, low contrast on the fishing fight silhouette, and desktop mouse/keyboard prompts appearing on phone.

Top fixes:
1. Center the Baiting Bench panel and clear the top HUD and corner trim (u06-bench on desktop 1280 and phone 667): set AnchorPoint to (0.5, 0.5) and Position to UDim2.fromScale(0.5, 0.54) on desktop - and hide the top HUD bar while the bench is open on phone (matching Shop and Journal) so it stops overlapping the HUD bar; add at least 28 phone px left inset and 14 phone px bottom padding so 'Tip: Thread it on...' clears the bottom-left leather corner patch, and raise 'point / bend / shank' and 'Tip:' text to at least 14 phone px.
2. Fix clipped and colliding text on the Catch Card and Shop (u04-card-rare on phone 667; u02-shop on desktop 1280 and phone 667): on u04-card-rare (667), enlarge the hanging weight tag height by ~12 phone px (to at least 48 phone px) or constrain line spacing/TextSize (12–14 phone px) so '5 oz' is no longer cut off beneath '3 lb'; on u02-shop (1280 and 667), move 'You have 174 coins. Forecast: Clear and calm.' into a dedicated bottom footer strip with 36 desktop px / 28 phone px horizontal padding (or center-aligned) so it never overlaps the bottom-left stitched leather corner.
3. Rebalance Fight screen hierarchy, contrast, and touch controls (u05-fight on desktop 1280 and phone 667; u01-hud and u02-shop on phone 667): shrink the oversized 'REELING!' banner into a compact ~220x48 desktop px (~180x36 phone px) prompt so the meter leads the eye and the lake stays visible; brighten the green catch zone (e.g., soft sage #9BB594) or add a cream outline to the dark fish icon for instant contrast; swap mouse/keyboard strings ('Hold click', 'Journal (J)', 'Hold the mouse anywhere, or Space.', 'Drag: Medium (Q)') for touch wording on mobile, and increase left HUD tabs, Shop Close/Buy buttons, and the Fight Drag button to at least 44 phone px tall.

Strengths:
- Strong handmade field-guide and tackle-box identity across almost every screen: textured cream paper cards, stitched leather headers and corner patches, woven canvas backing, serif titles, handwriting notes, and charming custom HUD icons (coin, day/night dial, wicker creel, worm tin, journal).
- Clean clearance of the Roblox top bar across all six phone (-667) and desktop (-1280) captures; nothing sits under or touches Roblox's top-left buttons.
- The rare catch card (u04-card-rare-1280) is a standout piece of UI craft, combining a hanging manila weight tag, ruler ticks, stamped 'RARE' ink mark, and clear field-guide typography.
- High desktop text legibility overall, with dark ink on warm cream paper keeping text clearly separated from the 3D lake and sky.

Broken or ugly:
- u06-bench-1280 and u06-bench-667: The 'Bait the hook: Worms' panel is visibly shifted off-center to the left and its top edge overlaps the bottom of the top HUD bar ('174 | 12:00 PM | 2 fish').
- u04-card-rare-667: The hanging manila weight tag clips off the second line ('5 oz'), showing only '3 lb' instead of '3 lb 5 oz'.
- u02-shop-1280 and u02-shop-667: The bottom status text ('You have 174 coins. Forecast: Clear and calm.') collides directly with the dark brown stitched leather corner patch at bottom-left, harming readability.
- u06-bench-667: The bottom 'Tip: Thread it on two or three times and leave the tail wiggling.' line touches/overlaps the bottom-left leather corner patch.
- u03-journal-1280 and u03-journal-667: The three bottom buttons ('Reel assist: Off', 'Quick Bait: On', 'Tackle Box') are left-aligned with an awkward empty gap on the right, and the scrollbar thumb floats up alongside the subtitle text (shrinking to a tiny isolated dot on 667).
- u01-hud-667 and u05-fight-667: Desktop-only controls ('Journal (J)', 'Hold click and let go to cast.', 'Hold the mouse anywhere, or Space.', 'Drag: Medium (Q)') are shown on the phone layout.
- u05-fight-1280 and u05-fight-667: Dark charcoal fish silhouette sits on a dark muted green-grey catch bar with low contrast, while the oversized 'REELING!' card dominates the right side of the screen.
- u06-bench-1280 and u06-bench-667: The baiting worm graphic (floating pink spheres on a flat teal rectangle) looks noticeably placeholder compared to the polished illustrated icons elsewhere.
