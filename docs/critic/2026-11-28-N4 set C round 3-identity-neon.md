The hero boats, weathered timber boathouse, wooden docks, and golden-hour/dawn lighting already give this game a warm, authentic northern-lodge identity that rises well above default Roblox. What still breaks the illusion - especially under bright midday light in c02-slips-boathouse-noon.jpg - is the backdrop: flat-shaded low-poly trees, stock Roblox smooth terrain along the shoreline, default midday sky and clouds, smooth plastic-like shoreline rocks, and an untextured blocky placeholder boat visible in the background of c03-dinghy-wake-dawn.jpg.
