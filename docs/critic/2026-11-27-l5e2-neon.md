**Score: 6/10**

The chunky low-poly style, cosy dockside stalls, and pastel dawn and golden-hour skies already give this fishing spot real charm, especially around the cattail weedbed and sunken logs. What holds it back from feeling like a memorable, living lake are a broken night Atmosphere that makes distant hills glow brighter than the sky, lifeless night water, flat noon lighting, bare ditch-like banks at Cedar Creek, and several clipped signs and rough terrain edges.
