**Score: 5.5/10**

The interface has a genuinely charming, cohesive field-guide and tackle-box identity that avoids stock Roblox simulator tropes, with warm cream paper cards, stitched leather, canvas textures, brass buttons, and illustrated HUD icons. However, severe layout, anchoring, padding, and state-visibility bugs currently break multiple screens - most notably clipping the Catch Card and REELING! banner off the right side at 1280x720, leaving the cast prompt visible behind the fight UI, overflowing text on the left HUD tabs and journal cards, and shrinking handwritten notes below readable size on the 667x375 phone layout.

Strengths:
- Authentic handmade field-guide and tackle-box aesthetic across screens: the top HUD strip (brass fish coin, sun/moon dial, woven creel), canvas-and-leather Bait and Tackle shop, paper Fish Journal, and blue-ink RARE stamp with hanging 3 lb tag feel custom-crafted rather than stock Roblox.
- Strong visual hierarchy on key screens: Bait and Tackle cleanly separates serif item titles, sans descriptions, and brass price buttons, while the Catch Card draws the eye immediately to Largemouth Bass and the RARE stamp.
- Consistent colour palette and typography pairing (warm cream paper, dark ink, brown leather, muted brass, serif headers, clean sans labels) that harmonises well with the calm lakeside 3D scene without cluttering the view of the water.
