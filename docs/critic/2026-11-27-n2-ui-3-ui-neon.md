**Score: 7/10**

This interface already looks genuinely handcrafted and distinct, strongly hitting the field-guide and tackle-box theme with warm cream paper, stitched leather, woven canvas, brass buttons, and charming custom icons - and every screen cleanly clears the Roblox top bar on both desktop and phone. The main things holding it back from an 8 or 9 are concrete layout bugs (text overlapping the dark leather corner patch on the Shop footer, the Bench modal sitting noticeably off-center to the left, left-aligned footer buttons in the Journal), PC-only control hints appearing on phone captures, slightly undersized ~37-38 phone px touch buttons and sub-14 phone px secondary labels, and a scattered Fight screen whose oversized REELING banner steals focus from the reel gauge.

Strengths:
- Strong handmade field-guide and tackle-box identity across almost every screen: stitched leather headers and corner triangles, canvas backgrounds, cream paper cards with soft shadows, and illustrated HUD icons (coin, sun/moon dial, wicker creel, worm bucket, journal, checklist).
- u04-card-rare is a standout centerpiece on both desktop and phone: crisp serif title, hanging manila weight tag on a string, ruler graphic, and tilted blue RARE ink stamp while leaving the lake and bobber visible.
- Clean Roblox top-bar clearance on all 6 desktop (1519x787) and phone (667x375) captures - no HUD element or modal touches or hides behind the top-left Roblox buttons.
- Calm, breathable HUD (u01-hud) that keeps the water and environment front and center without dark simulator boxes or neon clutter.
