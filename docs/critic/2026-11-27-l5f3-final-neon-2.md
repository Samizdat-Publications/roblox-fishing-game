**Score: 7.2/10**

The lake has genuine charm at dawn and golden hour, with inviting teal water, readable fishing spots like the lily-pad weedbed and sunken logs, and much better-proportioned dock lamp pools. The main things holding the look back are the inverted bright-hills-on-black-sky night atmosphere, Cedar Creek's overly smooth ditch-like riffle and sparse banks, and a few UI and prop placement clashes around the new trophy wall and stall signs.

Top fixes:
1. Fix night Atmosphere and moonlight on the water (04-dockend-night, 12-pool-night): at night, lower Atmosphere.Haze and shift Atmosphere.Color and Decay to deep midnight navy so the distant hills stop glowing paler than the black sky, and aim a dim cool moonlight directional light across the lake so the water keeps subtle surface glints instead of turning flat dark navy.
2. Build out Cedar Creek's riffle and pool banks (09-riffle-dawn, 10-pool-golden): widen the riffle slightly and place half-submerged low-poly river rocks, gravel patches, and a subtle white foam/current effect in the stream so it reads as a real rocky riffle instead of a smooth blue ditch; add more clustered reeds, boulders, and a couple of overhanging trees or bushes along both banks of the creek pool.
3. Unblock the trophy wall and polish BillboardGui signs (01-dock-dawn, 08-stands-noon, 11-gate-noon): move the green chalkboard or reposition the new trophy wall beside Your Pond so the chalkboard and cattails no longer hide it from the dock; add UIPadding inside the dark sign pills (Bait and Tackle and Cedar Creek nearly touch their pill borders), give Your Pond the same dark pill style for phone contrast, and shrink or slightly lower the stall BillboardGuis so they do not slice off at the screen edges as l Fish and Bait and T.

Strengths:
- Golden hour and dawn lighting (02-dock-golden, 05-weedbed-golden, 06-logs-dawn, 10-pool-golden) create warm, memorable moods with lovely peach and lavender reflections dancing across the rippled teal water.
- Micro-habitats read immediately for fishing gameplay: the cattails and pink water lilies in 05-weedbed-golden and the half-submerged logs in 06-logs-dawn make you want to cast right beside them.
- The smaller dock lamp glow seen in 07-overview-golden and the soft bank lamp pool illuminating the reeds in 12-pool-night feel restrained and atmospheric rather than washed out.
- Chunky low-poly props like the dock planks, colour-coded stalls, and Cedar Creek gate (08-stands-noon, 11-gate-noon) keep silhouettes clean and legible for both desktop and phone screens.
- Adding the first reeds and bank rock to the creek pool (10-pool-golden) is a good step toward breaking up the shoreline.

Broken or ugly:
- Night sky haze inversion in 04-dockend-night and 12-pool-night makes the distant hills look much brighter than the jet-black starry sky behind them.
- In 08-stands-noon, the new trophy wall is heavily occluded from the dock by the green chalkboard and foreground cattails directly in front of it.
- In 01-dock-dawn and 02-dock-golden, the Sell Fish and Bait and Tackle BillboardGuis clip awkwardly at the left and right edges of the viewport, and in 06-logs-dawn a stray green letter d from the Your Pond label peeks onto the far left edge.
- In 08-stands-noon, Your Pond uses plain unbacked text while Sell Fish, Bait and Tackle, and Cedar Creek use dark rounded pills with almost no side padding.
- In 09-riffle-dawn, the stream riffle reads as a straight, flat-bottomed ditch cut through the grass with no rocks or water texture variation.
