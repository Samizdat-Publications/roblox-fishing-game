**Score: 7.2/10**

This build already nails a cozy, authentic pond atmosphere - especially at dusk in dinghy-dusk-2.mp4, where the warm boathouse lantern, dark reflective water, and distant red buoy make you immediately want to drop a line. The parchment HUD and contextual boating warnings are thoughtful and clear. The main things holding this footage back are the lingering boat wake while stopped (00:08-00:12), a far-too-tight camera in the canoe (01.jpg, 04.jpg), an overly marbled daytime water reflection, and mobile HUD sizing/placement. Note: no casting, bite waiting, or catch reveal appears in the clip or stills, so suspense and reveal could not be scored.

Top fixes:
1. Pull back and slightly raise the canoe camera (01.jpg, 04.jpg) to match the dinghy's framing so the player's torso and hat stop blocking the center of the view and overlapping the bottom edge of the top HUD bar.
2. Tie the dinghy's V-wake trails and motor foam opacity/emission directly to boat velocity so they fade out completely when stopped to cast (dinghy-dusk-2.mp4 00:08-00:12), and soften the hard straight edges of the wake ribbons.
3. Adapt the HUD for small phones: raise or compact the left-side tabs (Worms, Journal, Goals) so they clear a mobile virtual thumbstick, bump up the font size on secondary labels ('worth 14', 'Clear', warning banners, bottom hints), and swap desktop prompts ('W and S', 'Hold click', '(J)', '[E]') for touch labels on mobile.

Strengths:
- Strong dusk mood and lighting in dinghy-dusk-2.mp4 (00:00-00:12), with warm lantern glow on the wooden boathouse, rich purple twilight sky, dark glassy water, and a glowing red buoy in the cove.
- Genuinely inviting Real-ish environment in 01.jpg and 04.jpg: weathered wood siding, dock pilings, reeds/cattails, varied tree silhouettes, and the 'Your Pond' sign make it look like a real place you want to fish.
- Cohesive, handcrafted parchment HUD (top status bar and left wooden-post tabs) that reads cleanly on desktop and fits the theme without looking like stock Roblox UI.
- Clear, proactive boating guidance in dinghy-dusk-2.mp4: obstacle and shore banners ('Something is in the way. Turn the boat.' at 00:03-00:04; 'Shore ahead. Turn the boat, or land here.' at 00:05-00:06) plus timely prompts to tie up, land, or cast.
- Good third-person camera distance in the dinghy (dinghy-dusk-2.mp4), giving a wide, readable view of the cove and nearby docks.

Broken or ugly:
- Dinghy wake lines and white foam behind the outboard motor stay visibly painted on the water for 4+ seconds after the boat stops moving and enters cast mode (dinghy-dusk-2.mp4 00:08-00:12); while moving (00:01-00:07), the wake trails also have stiff, straight edges.
- Canoe camera in 01.jpg and 04.jpg is positioned too close and low, filling the middle of the frame with the avatar's back/hat and causing the hat (and fishing rod in 04.jpg) to overlap the top HUD bar.
- A huge flat bright-white moon disc peeks out behind the top-left of the HUD bar in dinghy-dusk-2.mp4 (00:00, 00:01, 00:04), reading like a stray white UI artifact instead of a natural moon.
- Daytime water reflections in 01.jpg and 04.jpg look overly glossy and marbled (almost oil-slick) compared to the far more natural dusk water in dinghy-dusk-2.mp4.
- The floating boat-shop price signs ('350', '1,200', 'Coming Soon' in 04.jpg) feel bulky in the scenic cove and visually overlap one another from boating angles.
