Top fixes:
1. Overhaul water settings and riffle foam: in Workspace.Terrain, shift WaterColor from muddy brown toward a deeper muted green-teal, raise WaterReflectance (0.5 to 0.8) and lower WaterWaveSize so the pool catches sky and moonlight instead of looking like swirled mud, and replace the hard-edged rectangular whitewater planes in 09-riffle-dawn.jpg and the far left of 12-pool-night.jpg with soft-feathered alpha decals or tapered Beams.
2. Rebalance Lighting and Atmosphere for readability across all times of day: increase OutdoorAmbient and EnvironmentDiffuseScale at dawn (09-riffle-dawn.jpg) and night (12-pool-night.jpg) with a cool moonlight fill so shorelines and water edges do not crush to black on phone screens; dim the night lantern slightly to stop blowing out the path; add Atmosphere Haze and higher shadow ambient at noon (11-gate-noon.jpg) to soften the pitch-black tree shadows and harsh bright grass.
3. Ground the path and props: repaint the flat smooth grey-white path on the right side of the pool (10-pool-golden.jpg and 12-pool-night.jpg) using rough gravel, dirt, and blended grass edges; vary the scale, rotation, and spacing of the cloned pine trees ringing the pool; clear the giant bush blocking the bottom-left foreground in 09-riffle-dawn.jpg and move the lantern post slightly apart from the right timber post of the Cedar Creek gate in 11-gate-noon.jpg.

- Fireflies drifting over the water and the warm lantern glow in 12-pool-night.jpg give the night pool real atmosphere and make the bank feel cozy.
- The Cedar Creek wooden entrance gate in 11-gate-noon.jpg has convincing wood grain, a crisp readable font, and strong park-sign identity that works on both desktop and phone.
