**Score: 7.5/10**

The interface already nails its handmade field-guide and tackle-box identity with warm cream cards, stitched leather, woven canvas, brass buttons, and charming custom icons, and every screen cleanly clears the Roblox top bar. However, a handful of concrete layout and clipping bugs - especially the off-center Bait Bench overlapping the top HUD, footer text colliding with stitched leather corners, the clipped weight tag on phone, and an oversized REELING banner - need cleanup to make desktop and phone feel equally polished.

Top fixes:
1. u06-bench (desktop 1519x787 and phone 667x375): Center the Bait Bench panel horizontally (AnchorPoint 0.5, 0.5; Position Scale X 0.5) and hide the top HUD while it is open (or push the panel top edge below 96 desktop px / 90 phone px) so it no longer overlaps the top HUD; add 28 desktop px / 24 phone px of left padding and 8 px bottom padding so 'Tip: Thread it on...' clears the bottom-left leather corner, and raise 'point / bend / shank' and the tip line to at least 14 phone px.
2. u04-card-rare (phone 667x375) and u02-shop (desktop and phone): On u04-card-rare-667, increase the hanging weight tag height by 14 phone px (or adjust text size to 13 phone px with tighter line height) so '5 oz' is no longer clipped off below '3 lb'; on u02-shop (both sizes), shift the footer 'You have 174 coins. Forecast: Clear and calm.' right by 32 desktop px / 26 phone px and up by 8 px so it clears the bottom-left stitched leather corner patch.
3. u05-fight (desktop and phone) plus touch prompts on u01-hud-667 and u05-fight-667: Shrink the oversized 'REELING!' banner on u05-fight to a compact ~240x68 desktop px / ~170x52 phone px strip so visual hierarchy stays on the left catch meter, raise the Drag button height to 46 phone px, and swap desktop-only text ('Hold click', 'Journal (J)', 'Hold the mouse anywhere, or Space.', 'Drag: Medium (Q)') for touch-friendly phrasing when touch input is active.

Strengths:
- Handmade field-guide and tackle-box aesthetic is cohesive and distinctive: stitched leather headers and corner patches, woven canvas, warm paper cards, embossed brass buttons, serif titles, clean sans body text, and handwritten notes avoid any stock simulator look.
- The u04-card-rare catch card is a standout, combining a framed 3D fish preview, tick-mark ruler, hanging weight tag, and angled blue ink RARE stamp with clear typographic hierarchy.
- Top HUD icons (fish coin, day/night dial, wicker creel) and left-rail icons (bait bucket, journal, goals) are crisp, immediately legible on both desktop and 667x375 phone, and completely clear the Roblox top bar on all six screens.
- Core desktop readability and text-to-background contrast are strong across paper cards and 3D water scenes, and most phone touch targets (left HUD tabs, journal footer buttons, hook-size buttons) sit comfortably around 44 to 48 phone pixels tall.

Broken or ugly:
- u06-bench-1280 and u06-bench-667: The Bait Bench panel overlaps the bottom edge of the top HUD bar and is shifted noticeably off-center to the left.
- u04-card-rare-667: The hanging paper weight tag clips out the second line ('5 oz'), displaying only '3 lb' instead of '3 lb 5 oz'.
- u02-shop-1280 and u02-shop-667: The bottom footer text ('You have 174 coins. Forecast: Clear and calm.') collides directly with the dark brown bottom-left stitched leather corner patch and bottom border.
- u06-bench-1280 and u06-bench-667: The bottom line ('Tip: Thread it on two or three times and leave the tail wiggling.') touches and overlaps the bottom-left leather corner patch, and on -667 the 'point / bend / shank' labels and tip text drop under 14 phone px.
- u03-journal-1280 and u03-journal-667: The bottom row of three buttons ('Reel assist: Off', 'Quick Bait: On', 'Tackle Box') is left-aligned, leaving an awkward empty gap on the right side of the page, and uses plain cream outlines that look flat compared to the brass and leather buttons elsewhere.
- u01-hud-667 and u05-fight-667: Desktop keyboard and mouse prompts ('Hold click and let go to cast.', 'Journal (J)', 'Hold the mouse anywhere, or Space.', 'Drag: Medium (Q)') appear on the phone layout.
- u06-bench-1280 and u06-bench-667: The worm in the hook-baiting minigame reads as disconnected pink spheres on a flat solid blue box, looking placeholder-like next to the illustrated icons and textured materials elsewhere.
