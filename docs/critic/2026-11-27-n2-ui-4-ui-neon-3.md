**Score: 7.5/10**

The interface already nails its handmade field-guide and tackle-box identity, with warm cream paper cards, stitched leather trim, brass buttons, custom illustrated icons, and strong contrast over the 3D lake. Every phone screen cleanly clears the Roblox top bar and keeps the water visible. What holds the UI back from a 9 is a set of HUD/modal state overlaps (especially the bite banner colliding with the fight meter on desktop and the Fish button remaining open beside the baiting bench on phone), cramped sub-14px helper text and slightly undersized touch targets on phone, and a placeholder-looking worm-and-hook diagram on the baiting bench.
