**Score: 6/10**

The game already has genuine charm, lovely dawn and golden hour skies, and clear fishing habitats like the cattail weedbed and sunken logs, which fit the calm low-poly goal well. Its biggest drawback is that the main lake, Cedar Creek riffle, and pool are built as rigid 90-degree rectangles with ruler-straight paths, making them feel like man-made tanks rather than real fishing waters, alongside clipped floating signs and poor night bobber visibility.

Top fixes:
1. Reshape the straight 90-degree shorelines of the main lake, Cedar Creek riffle, and pool (07, 09, 10) into organic curved banks with coves, gently sloped dirt-and-pebble edges, and shoreline reeds/boulders/trees, and curve or taper the ruler-straight beige path instead of boxing in the water.
2. Retune water and night lighting: shift Terrain WaterColor from bright swimming-pool cyan (03, 08) to a deeper freshwater slate-teal, fix the straight water seam in 10-pool-golden.jpg, add a glowing neon tip or tiny light to the bobber so it is readable at night (04), and tighten the dock and path PointLight ranges with warmer amber color so they stop creating large murky-green glow blobs in the water (04, 12).
3. Fix all clipped BillboardGui labels by raising their StudsOffset cleanly above the roofs and beams - or replacing them with mounted wood SurfaceGui signs - so Cedar Creek (11) and Sell Fish / Bait and Tackle (01, 02, 08) never get cut off by geometry.

Strengths:
- Dawn and golden hour lighting (01, 02, 05, 06, 10) produces genuinely warm skies, pleasing distant hill silhouettes, and attractive pink-and-amber water glints.
- Distinct real-world fishing cover - the cattail and lily-pad weedbed in 05-weedbed-golden.jpg and the submerged logs and boulders in 06-logs-dawn.jpg - immediately invites players to read the water and pick spots.
- The chunky low-poly dock planks, striped Bait and Tackle and Sell Fish stands, rod rack, and hanging scale (08-stands-noon.jpg) look cohesive, handcrafted, and easy to read even on a small phone screen.
- Uncluttered camera views from the dock toward the water (01, 02, 03) give a calm, clear sightline with strong noon bobber readability.
