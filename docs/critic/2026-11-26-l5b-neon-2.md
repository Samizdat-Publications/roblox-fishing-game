**Score: 5.5/10**

This already has real charm and the right instincts for a thoughtful fishing game: dawn and golden-hour skies look lovely, the chunky low-poly stands and dock feel welcoming, and distinct fishing structures like sunken logs and reed beds clearly signal where fish should hold. What holds it back most right now is that every body of water is a sharp 90-degree rectangular box or trench set into flat lawn, alongside a few harsh lighting transitions at noon and night and an unfinished wooden barrier wall at Cedar Creek.

Top fixes:
1. Sculpt natural, irregular shorelines to remove the rectangular swimming-pool look: use Roblox Terrain sculpt and smooth tools to curve the edges of the main lake, pool, and Cedar Creek riffle (07-overview-golden, 09-riffle-dawn, 10-pool-golden), add gentle sloping banks instead of vertical drop-offs, replace the rigid straight beige perimeter path with an organic dirt trail that pulls back from the water, and cluster varied low-poly rocks, bushes, overhanging trees, and uneven reed beds along the banks.
2. Rework Terrain Water and time-of-day lighting for noon and night: shift Terrain WaterColor from bright pool cyan (03-dockend-noon, 08-stands-noon) to a deeper natural lake blue-green, add soft Terrain Clouds and warmer directional sunlight so noon has depth and shadow contrast, dial back the dock PointLight Range or switch to downward SpotLights to eliminate the glowing cyan water floor at night (04-dockend-night), and give the bobber a subtle night-time glow so players can still track bites on a phone.
3. Replace placeholder boundaries, fix the water seam, and clean up world UI signs: swap the flat wooden plank wall and mismatched gate posts at Cedar Creek (11-gate-noon) for a natural low-poly rail fence, tree line, or wooden footbridge, remove the overlapping water part or terrain seam cutting across the pool (10-pool-golden, 12-pool-night), raise the Stand BillboardGui StudsOffset so signs do not clip behind roofs from behind (01-dock-dawn, 02-dock-golden), and change the dark blue Bait and Tackle text (08-stands-noon) to high-contrast white or light cream with a dark text stroke.

Strengths:

- Distinct fishing habitats are immediately legible: the main dock, weedbeds with cattails and a visible striped fish (05-weedbed-golden), sunken logs (06-logs-dawn), and a creek riffle and pool give players genuine environmental cues for choosing spots.
- The chunky low-poly stands, striped awnings, rod rack, hanging scale, dock planks, and silhouetted pine hills (01-dock-dawn, 08-stands-noon) have a clean, cohesive style that reads well at a glance.
