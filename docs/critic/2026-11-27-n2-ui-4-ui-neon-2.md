**Score: 7.5/10**

The handmade field-guide and tackle-box aesthetic is already genuinely charming and cohesive, with crisp desktop readability, warm paper-and-leather craft, and clean clearance below the Roblox top bar on both desktop and phone. The main things holding it back are concrete layout collisions on the Fight and Bait Bench screens, a few undersized text labels and touch targets on the 667 phone size, and placeholder-looking worm art inside the Bait Bench.

1. u05-fight (desktop 1519x787 and phone 666x374): Hide the bottom 'Bite! Press click now!' prompt banner as soon as the reel meter activates so it stops colliding behind the meter's bottom-right corner on desktop; replace or shrink the oversized right-hand 'REELING!' card (or omit it on phone where 'Hold to reel' and the circular 'REEL' button already explain the control) so it stops blocking the lake and contradicting the REEL button; raise the phone 'Drag: Medium' button height from ~38 to 46 phone px.

3. u01-hud, u02-shop, u03-journal (phone 666x374 and desktop 1519x787): On phone, bump undersized body/subtext labels from ~11-12 phone px to at least 14 phone px (HUD 'Clear'/'worth 0', Shop item descriptions, Journal summary line and species details) and increase touch target heights from ~38 to 44-48 phone px on the three left HUD tabs (u01-hud-667) and Shop/Journal Close buttons (u02-shop-667, u03-journal-667); on desktop, hide the HUD status toast while modals are open so it stops peeking behind the top edge of Shop and Journal.

Strengths:
- Strong handmade identity across HUD, Shop, Journal, and Catch Card: warm cream paper, stitched leather headers and corner patches, custom brass/creel/dial/journal icons, ruler ticks, hanging paper weight tag, and a tilted blue RARE ink stamp.
- Every single phone capture cleanly clears the Roblox top-left bar with breathing room and keeps the lake and treeline visible behind the HUD.
- Desktop readability and ink-on-paper contrast are very strong across the HUD, Shop, Journal, and Catch Card, with clean removal of keyboard hints like (J) and (Q) on phone.
- Smart phone adaptations on Shop and Journal that hide the side HUD and Fish button when modals open, plus generous 82-phone-px circular Fish and REEL touch buttons and 44-phone-px Journal bottom controls.
