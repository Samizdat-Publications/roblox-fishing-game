**Score: 5.5/10**

The main lake has real charm at dawn and golden hour - the chunky low-poly stands, warm dock lanterns, misty hills, and readable fishing zones like the weedbed and sunken logs already feel cozy and promising. What holds the game back right now is the rigid, boxy geometry of the paths and Cedar Creek (which looks like straight ditches and a rectangular swimming pool), flat shadowless noon lighting, muddy green light blobs in the night water, and several floating signs clipping into wood beams and trees.

Top fixes:
1. Replace the ruler-straight shorelines and paths with organic terrain curves: in Roblox Terrain Editor, use Erode and Smooth to wind Cedar Creek's riffle (09-riffle-dawn.jpg) and round out the sharp 90-degree corners of the Cedar Creek pool (10-pool-golden.jpg) and Your Pond (07-overview-golden.jpg), break up the square tan border path around the main lake with curving painted dirt trails, and dress the creek banks with low-poly boulders, reeds, and overhanging trees.
2. Upgrade lighting contrast and fix night readability: enable GlobalShadows with ShadowMap or Future lighting and lower OutdoorAmbient at noon so trees, stalls, and hills cast crisp directional shadows (03-dockend-noon.jpg, 08-stands-noon.jpg, 11-gate-noon.jpg); at night (04-dockend-night.jpg, 12-pool-night.jpg), tighten lamp ranges and lower Terrain.WaterTransparency so dock and path lights stop creating flat glowing-green blobs in the water, and add a small bright tip or subtle PointLight to the bobber so it reads on a phone screen.
3. Fix clipping signs and tune the water look: raise the StudsOffset on the Cedar Creek archway BillboardGui (11-gate-noon.jpg) or swap floating BillboardGuis for wooden SurfaceGui signboards on the gate and stall roofs so text stops clipping behind beams and awnings (01-dock-dawn.jpg, 02-dock-golden.jpg, 08-stands-noon.jpg), shift Terrain.WaterColor from bright tropical turquoise toward a deeper natural lake blue-green, and eliminate the straight water seam cutting across 10-pool-golden.jpg.

Strengths:
- Soft pink dawn and warm golden-hour skies with atmospheric haze over the distant hills (01-dock-dawn.jpg, 02-dock-golden.jpg, 06-logs-dawn.jpg) create an inviting lakeside mood.
- The chunky low-poly props - striped market awnings, hanging fish scale, rod rack, wooden plank dock, and lanterns (08-stands-noon.jpg, 07-overview-golden.jpg) - have a cohesive, charming style that reads well at small sizes.
- The main lake already communicates real fishing spots visually: the foreground cattails and lily pads in 05-weedbed-golden.jpg and the submerged logs and boulder in 06-logs-dawn.jpg immediately tell a player where to cast.
- The symmetrical framing between the two stands looking straight down the dock toward the lake and hills (01-dock-dawn.jpg, 02-dock-golden.jpg) makes a strong first impression.
