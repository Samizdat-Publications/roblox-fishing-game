**Score: 4/10**

A promising early prototype with genuine chunky low-poly charm on the wooden dock, market stalls, and distinct fishing habitats (buoy, cattail weedbed, sunken logs, and creek), plus lovely pastel dawn and golden-hour sky gradients. Right now, though, the map reads like a flat square swimming pool floating in an empty void, with harsh underwater shelf seams, an open creek edge dropping into the sky, glowing cyan night-water hotspots, and oversized floating signs that overlap and clip through props.

Top fixes:
1. Break up the rectangular swimming-pool borders and empty void horizon: curve the lake banks and creek channel using Smooth Terrain or rotated low-poly bank/rock wedges, close off the open void drop-off at the front of Cedar Creek (09-riffle-dawn.jpg) with boulders and a hillside, and surround the map perimeter with varied-height low-poly hills, pine/deciduous tree clusters, and a Lighting Atmosphere (Density around 0.3 to 0.4) to ground the horizon.
2. Replace or constrain the giant floating BillboardGuis (Sell Fish, Bait and Tackle, Your Pond, Cedar Creek): set MaxDistance to around 40-60 studs so labels vanish from across the lake (fixing the pile-up in 06 and 07), and either raise StudsOffset cleanly above roofs or switch to wooden SurfaceGui signboards mounted on the stall fronts and a proper trail arch at Cedar Creek so text never clips behind posts or walls (08, 11).
3. Clean up underwater seams and rebalance night lighting: replace the sharp rectangular submerged shelf edges visible through the water in 03, 05, 06, and 10 with gently sloped terrain and darker natural lakebed tones; warm and soften the dock lantern PointLights (warm amber tint, smaller range/brightness) to remove the glowing cyan disc in 04 and 07; and raise night moonlight/OutdoorAmbient plus add a soft lantern near Cedar Creek pool so 12-pool-night.jpg is readable and atmospheric instead of pitch dark.

Strengths:
- The chunky low-poly craft on the wooden dock planks, yellow/blue-striped market stalls, rod rack, cattails, and sunken logs in 01, 05, 06, and 08 looks cohesive and charming.
- The fishing spot layout already communicates real angling variety at a glance: open deep water by the buoy, shallow cattail cover, submerged timber, and a separate riffle/pool creek.
- Dawn and golden-hour skies in 01, 02, 05, and 06 cast pleasing pink and warm amber reflections across the water surface.
- Strong, clean silhouettes on the player, stalls, buoy, and reeds will scale down well for small phone screens once UI clutter is fixed.
