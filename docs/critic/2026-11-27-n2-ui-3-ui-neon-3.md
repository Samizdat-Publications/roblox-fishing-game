**Score: 7.5/10**

The field-guide and tackle-box art direction is genuinely cohesive and full of charm, with warm cream paper cards, stitched leather and canvas framing, brass buttons, and thoughtful details like the sun-and-moon dial and catch-card ruler. Nothing collides with the Roblox top bar on either desktop or phone. What holds the interface back are several concrete layout bugs (text overlapping leather corners on Shop and Bench, an off-center Bench panel, a flush-left Nice! tag), weak visual hierarchy and icon contrast on the Fight screen, and phone captures that still show desktop mouse/key prompts alongside 10-12 phone px text and ~38-40 phone px buttons.

Top fixes:
1. Fix overlaps and centering on u02-shop, u06-bench, and u03-journal (desktop 1519x787 and phone 667x375): on u02-shop, add at least 36 desktop px (24 phone px) left margin and 12 phone px bottom margin to the footer status label so it clears the bottom-left leather corner patch; on u06-bench, set the main panel AnchorPoint to (0.5, 0.5) and Position to UDim2.fromScale(0.5, 0.5) to center it, wrap the instruction text to the width of the timing bar, and move the bottom Tip label right by 24 desktop px (18 phone px) and up by 10 phone px; on u03-journal, size the three bottom buttons equally via UIListLayout (0.32 width scale each) so they fill the panel width cleanly.
2. Rebalance hierarchy, contrast, and mobile prompts on u05-fight and u01-hud (desktop and phone 667x375): on u05-fight, shrink the oversized right-hand REELING! card to a compact banner or dock it above the meter so the eye goes to the reeling gauge first, recolor the fish silhouette inside the gauge to warm cream (#F5EBD6) with a 2 px dark outline for >4.5:1 contrast against the dark green/blue bar, and center-align Nice! with 12 desktop px (8 phone px) horizontal padding; on phone (u01-hud-667 and u05-fight-667), detect touch input to strip keyboard hints like (J) and (Q) and replace Hold click / Hold the mouse anywhere, or Space with Hold anywhere to cast / Hold anywhere to reel.
3. Raise phone typography and touch targets to mobile minimums on phone (667x375) across u01-hud, u02-shop, u03-journal, u05-fight, and u06-bench: bump secondary labels currently around 10-12 phone px (HUD worth 22 and Clear, Shop item descriptions and footer, Journal species notes, Fight meter labels, and Bench point/bend/shank and Tip) to at least 14 phone px, and increase button heights from ~38-40 phone px to at least 44 phone px on HUD left tabs, Shop Buy and Close buttons, Journal Close button, Fight Drag button, and Bench Skip button.

Strengths:
- Handmade field-guide and tackle-box aesthetic lands strongly across all six screens with warm cream paper, stitched leather, canvas textures, embossed brass buttons, serif headers, and clean body type.
- Catch card (u04-card-rare) is a standout piece: the hanging weight tag with twine, printed inch ruler, and tilted blue RARE ink stamp feel like an authentic keepsake.
- HUD (u01-hud) stays unobtrusive on both desktop and phone, leaving the lake and bobber clear while cleanly clearing the Roblox top bar across every screen.
- Shop (u02-shop) and Journal (u03-journal) use calm spacing and readable dark-ink-on-cream cards that make browsing bait prices and species notes comfortable on desktop.
- Journal hides the top HUD bar on phone (u03-journal-667) and Shop does the same (u02-shop-667), giving modals vertical room to breathe without touching the Roblox top bar.

Broken or ugly:
- u02-shop (desktop and phone): Footer text You have 174 coins. Forecast: Clear and calm. overlaps directly over the bottom-left stitched leather corner patch, obscuring the word You.
- u06-bench (desktop and phone): The entire baiting panel is noticeably shifted off-center to the left (roughly 125 desktop px and 72 phone px left of center).
- u06-bench (desktop and phone): The bottom Tip line crowds and nearly touches the bottom-left leather corner, the instruction line spills past the left timing-bar column into the right column, and the worm made of disconnected pink spheres looks placeholder-like.
- u05-fight (desktop and phone): The Nice! label sits flush against the left border of its cream strip with no left padding and the right half of the box empty; the dark charcoal fish silhouette has low contrast against the dark teal-green target band and blue bar.
- u01-hud and u05-fight (phone 667x375): Desktop-only keyboard and mouse instructions appear on phone (Journal (J), Hold click and let go to cast., Hold the mouse anywhere, or Space., Drag: Medium (Q)).
- u04-card-rare (desktop and phone): The opaque cream background of the tilted RARE stamp clips off the right half of the circular blue fish seal underneath it; on phone (u04-card-rare-667) the hanging 3 lb 5 oz tag overlaps the bass's tail fin.
- u03-journal (desktop and phone): The three bottom buttons (Reel assist: Off, Quick Bait: On, Tackle Box) are left-aligned with awkward empty space on the right side.
