**Score: 5.5/10**

The interface already nails its target field-guide and tackle-box identity with warm cream paper, stitched leather, woven canvas, brass buttons, and charming bespoke icons that look nothing like stock Roblox UI. However, pervasive layout, anchoring, and padding bugs hold it back on almost every screen: the Rare catch card and REELING banner clip off the right edge on desktop, the catch portrait box is empty, the old cast prompt remains visible behind the fight UI, journal text straddles card borders, bench text overlaps leather corners, and phone text shrinks well below 14px while panels overlap the top HUD bar.

Top fixes:
1. Fix off-screen clipping, overlaps, and stray UI on the Catch Card and Fight screens (u04-card-rare-1280, u05-fight-1280, u05-fight-667): set the catch card AnchorPoint to (1, 0.5) at Position UDim2.new(1, -24, 0.55, 0) with a max width of 520px so the 3 lb tag, 18.1 in length, and RARE stamp stay on screen, and render the fish in the empty portrait ViewportFrame; hide the HUD cast prompt (Visible = false) when reeling begins; anchor the REELING banner to (1, 0.5) at UDim2.new(1, -20, 0.52, 0) so it stops clipping on 1280; shift the fight tension meter down (Position.Y = 0.58, max height 58% of screen) so it clears the top HUD bar on 667; make the Drag button at least 48px tall on 1280 and 44px on 667 with PaddingBottom = 10px so Line 8 lb clears the dark bevel; and remove or properly label the stray floating N tag.
2. Add UIPadding, AutomaticSize, and viewport height caps to stop text clipping and border collisions (u01-hud-1280/667, u02-shop-1280, u03-journal-1280/667, u06-bench-1280/667): widen the left HUD tabs to 200px on 1280 and 165px by 44px tall on 667 with left-aligned text and PaddingRight = 14px so Bait: Worms x17, Journal, and Goals 0/3 never clip; give the bottom cast prompt AutomaticSize = XY, TextWrapped = true, and 16px horizontal by 8px vertical UIPadding so the cream card expands around wrapped text; add 12px UIPadding inside every Fish Journal entry card with AutomaticSize = Y so species titles and habitat tips sit inside the frame borders; cap Shop and Journal modal heights to 78% of screen height centered below the top HUD bar so bottom edges and buttons are never cut off on 1280; and add 36px horizontal and 20px vertical UIPadding to the Bench canvas (shifting it down 18px on 667) so Bait the hook: Worms and the bottom Tip line never touch the stitched leather corners.
3. Enforce a 14px minimum phone font size and boost low-contrast elements (u02-shop-667, u03-journal-667, u04-card-rare-667, u05-fight-1280/667, u06-bench-1280/667): add UITextSizeConstraint with MinTextSize = 14 to item descriptions, the shop footer, journal summary and tips, catch card notes, and bench tips on 667 so nothing drops to 9-11px; give the Bench Skip action a 44px-tall cream or brass button with #2B2118 dark ink and darken the or hold 1 s label to #3D3024 for 4.5:1+ contrast; and recolour the dark fish silhouette inside the Fight tension bar to warm cream #F5EBD6 with a 2px dark outline so it pops cleanly against the blue-green track.

Strengths:
- Authentic handmade field-guide and tackle-box art direction across all screens, using stitched brown leather, tan woven canvas, warm cream paper cards, embossed brass buttons, a fish coin, a sun-and-moon clock dial, and a creel icon instead of flat dark simulator boxes.
- Strong desktop visual hierarchy in the Bait and Tackle shop (u02-shop-1280) and Bait the hook bench (u06-bench-1280), where serif titles, brass price buttons, and green-highlighted hook selection guide the eye immediately.
- Delightful bespoke details on the catch card (u04-card-rare-667), including the hanging 3 lb paper tag, measurement ruler, and angled blue RARE rubber ink stamp with fish seal.
- Clean, unobstructed view of the 3D lake and bobber on the HUD, catch card, and fight screens at both desktop and phone sizes.
